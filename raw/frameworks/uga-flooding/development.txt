Uganda — flood anticipatory action
Trigger design for a multi-zone flood framework: riverine flooding in Teso on a
         GloFAS point, rainfall-forecast triggers for flash floods and landslides on Mount
         Elgon and in Karamoja, and the Albert Nile around Adjumani — with an observed-flood
         backstop everywhere. Work in progress, September 2026.
