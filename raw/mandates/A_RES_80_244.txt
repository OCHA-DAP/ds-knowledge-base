                 C
                 Financing of appropriations for the year 2026
                        The General Assembly
                        Resolves that, for 2026:
                       1.   Budget appropriations totalling 3,461,385,200 United States dollars,
                 consisting of 3,450,426,300 dollars approved for 2026 by the General Assembly in
                 paragraph 1 of resolution A above, and 10,958,900 in additional appropriations for
                 2025, as approved by the Assembly in its resolution 79/258 B of 11 April 2025, shall
                 be financed in accordance with regulations 3.1 and 3.2 of the Financial Regulations
                 and Rules of the United Nations 1 and paragraph 3 of section XIV of resolution 80/243
                 of 30 December 2025 on the financial performance report on the programme budget
                 for 2024, as follows:
                      (a) 33,379,800 dollars, representing the estimated income other than staff
                 assessment income approved for 2026 under resolution B above;
                       (b) 3,428,005,400 dollars, representing the total to be apportioned among
                 Member States, of which an amount of 3,403,005,400 dollars is to be apportioned in
                 accordance with Assembly resolution 79/249 of 24 December 2024 on the scale of
                 assessments for the apportionment of the expenses of the United Nations. The
                 remaining amount of 25,000,000 dollars is to be apportioned in accordance with the
                 scale of assessments for peacekeeping operations of the United Nations for 2026, in
                 line with the decision of the Assembly reflected in its resolution 78/257 of
