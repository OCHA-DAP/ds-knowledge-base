# World Vision International — Timor-Leste drought

## Summary
A World Vision International anticipatory action framework for drought in Timor-Leste,
listed as active in the Anticipation Hub's global inventory since 2024. Unlike most other
anticipatory action activity in the country, this framework is coordinated **and**
implemented solely by World Vision — it is not part of a multi-agency consortium and not a
component of an OCHA/CERF collective framework (none exists for Timor-Leste). No public
document describing its trigger design, funding source, or target locations has been found;
the entry is currently sourced only from the Hub's inventory record.

## Trigger
Not publicly documented. No EAP, protocol summary, or project page describing the
indicator, threshold, or lead time for this framework was found (see `extra.schema_strain`).

## Funding & scope
US$144,302 pre-arranged, targeting 3,306 people, per the Anticipation Hub's global-map
inventory (2024 listing; the Hub reports this as an "investment" figure without a
source/donor breakdown). No municipality or village-level targeting is stated in the public
record found. This is separate from the better-documented Australia-funded "Disaster READY"
Anticipatory Action pilot that World Vision co-implements for **flood** in Bobonaro
municipality (see `extra.distinct_from`) — that funding and scope do not apply here.

## Activations
None known. The Hub's activations layer for Timor-Leste records only a 2023 drought
activation by FAO (a separate framework); no activation of this World Vision framework has
been reported publicly.
