# World Vision International — Bangladesh tropical cyclone

## Summary
World Vision Bangladesh (WVB) piloted a "Cyclone Anticipatory Action Protocol" as part of
a 2022-2023 ADH Germany-funded regional anticipatory-action project (also covering
Indonesia, Mongolia, Philippines, Myanmar, Sri Lanka), with the Bangladesh cyclone
component sited in coastal Bagerhat district (Rampal, Morelganj upazilas) alongside a
separate flood component in Jamalpur. No public document sets out a quantified forecast
trigger (indicator/threshold/lead time) for this protocol. Ahead of Cyclone Remal (May
2024), WVB scaled up preparedness and early-warning dissemination across coastal
districts (Satkhira, Khulna, Cox's Bazar), but this reads as general emergency
mobilisation rather than a documented, pre-arranged-financing trigger release. WVB is not
one of the implementing agencies or co-funders of the separate OCHA/CERF collective
Bangladesh cyclone framework.

## Trigger
No public World Vision document states a specific forecast indicator, wind-speed or BMD
signal threshold, or lead time. The ADH-funded pilot names BMD warnings as the basis for
community-level preparedness action in Bagerhat, and the 2024 Remal response describes
WVB teams and ~3,000 community volunteers acting on BMD cyclone warnings and disseminating
early-warning messages — but no quantified go/no-go rule was found in any source examined.

## Funding & scope
The pilot (Sep 2022-Nov 2023) was funded by ADH Germany; no total budget figure is stated
in the public project overview. It targeted a combined 10,000 direct / 60,000 indirect
beneficiaries across its two area programmes — Bagerhat (cyclone) and Jamalpur (flood) —
with no cyclone-only breakdown published, so no cyclone-specific `target_people` figure is
recorded here. No pre-arranged financing tranche or per-source funding breakdown was found
for the cyclone component specifically, nor for the 2024 Remal mobilisation.

## Activations
- **26 May 2024 — Cyclone Remal**: WVB National/Regional disaster-management teams on
  high alert, Emergency Operation Centres activated, ~3,000 volunteers and 1,400+
  community-based groups disseminating early-warning messages, ~600 dry food packs and
  ~450 hygiene kits distributed across Satkhira, Khulna and Cox's Bazar (including
  Rohingya camps and host communities) — part of the >1.3 million people in the storm's
  path. No stated trigger threshold or pre-arranged financing tranche.
- A frequently-repeated secondary-source claim that WVB "activated readiness measures" for
  Cyclone Mocha (May 2023) could not be traced to a primary, fetchable document and is
  therefore not recorded as a verified activation (see `extra.schema_strain`).
