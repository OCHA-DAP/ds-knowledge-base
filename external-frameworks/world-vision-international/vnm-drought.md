# World Vision International — Vietnam drought

## Summary
World Vision International's Vietnam drought work sits within FBEA-SEA ("Enhancing
Inclusive and Gender-responsive Forecast-based Early Action for Effectiveness Disaster
Preparedness in Vietnam and at Regional Level"), an ECHO-funded consortium project with
CARE and Plan International. Within the consortium, World Vision owns the drought
component — Thuận Hòa and Đông Tiến communes, Hàm Thuận Bắc district, Bình Thuận
province — while CARE leads a parallel flood/flash-flood component in Quảng Trị
province. The project built local capacity and forecast-based early-action standard
operating procedures rather than standing up an already-operational, pre-financed
trigger system.

## Trigger
Public sources describe the project's aim as helping communities and local authorities
"operationalise existing frameworks and guidelines into contextualised SOPs and
anticipatory action" for drought, drawing on satellite drought monitoring and climate
forecasting modelling produced by Vietnam's national hydro-meteorological institute
(IMHEN). No public document was found that states a specific indicator, numeric
threshold, or lead time for when drought early action would be triggered in Bình Thuận —
see `extra.schema_strain`.

## Funding & scope
- EUR 143,000 (~USD 173,030) of ECHO grant funding allocated specifically to World
  Vision Việt Nam's component (per World Vision's own project page).
- ~4,560 people targeted in Thuận Hòa and Đông Tiến communes, Hàm Thuận Bắc district,
  Bình Thuận province (World Vision's stated figure; the Anticipation Hub inventory
  record that seeded this page's stub separately gave 3,400 — see
  `extra.schema_strain`).
- Project timeline stated by World Vision as July 2021-December 2022 (18 months); the
  consortium's own project page was still describing it in the present tense as of
  September 2024 (see `extra.schema_strain` on the resulting status/`valid_until`
  uncertainty).
- Part of a wider consortium: CARE (flood/flash flood, Quảng Trị) and Plan
  International also implement components under the same ECHO grant.

## Activations
None known. The public record describes FBEA-SEA as a preparedness/capacity-building
project — developing SOPs and forecast-based early-action mechanisms — rather than one
with a documented, dated trigger-and-release history for drought in Bình Thuận.
