# World Vision International — Iraq drought

## Summary
World Vision Iraq ran a drought anticipatory action (AA) pilot in Hatra district, Ninewa
Governorate — one piece of a six-country pilot (Kenya, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq,
Syria, Lebanon) funded by Aktion Deutschland Hilft (ADH) and running October 2023 to
December 2024. The Iraq/Lebanon/Syria piece was implemented by World Vision Middle
East and Eastern Europe (WV MEER). The pilot combined early warning dissemination,
community/government capacity building, and pre-agreed cash for seed and fodder
purchase, targeting rural communities in Hatra exposed to recurrent drought and
wildfire. No numeric trigger design was published, and World Vision's own lessons-learned
report says the Iraq trigger simulation was run incorrectly — so the mechanism's
real-world reliability was never demonstrated.

## Trigger
Public materials describe the design only in general terms: activities were to be
released "according to the indicators and risks identified following feasibility
assessments and risk forecasting," developed in coordination with local communities and
government stakeholders (fact sheet). No document states the specific hazard indicator
(e.g. rainfall/SPI, NDVI), a numeric threshold, or a lead time. World Vision's regional
lessons-learned report (March 2025) states that trigger-mechanism and simulation
exercises were a project-wide weak point, and specifically that "in Iraq, the simulation
was mistimed and conducted in the wrong scenario setting, indicating a capacity gap" —
i.e. the one documented test of Iraq's trigger did not work as intended.

## Funding & scope
- Funded by Aktion Deutschland Hilft (ADH) as part of the regional MEER pilot (Oct
  2023–Dec 2024); no Iraq-specific budget figure appears in World Vision's own fact
  sheet or lessons-learned report.
- The Anticipation Hub's global map/Iraq country page records USD 33,557 pre-arranged
  funding and 7,657 people targeted for this framework (coordinating org: World Vision
  International) — these are Hub-inventory figures, not numbers stated in World Vision's
  own Iraq documents.
- Geographic scope: Hatra district, Ninewa Governorate.
- Activities: awareness raising in villages/schools, AA training for stakeholders,
  activation of early warning systems, and cash distribution for seed/fodder purchase
  ahead of drought/rain-scarcity seasons; separately, 75 farmers were trained in
  climate-resilient agriculture and 50 women in beekeeping/honey production as
  livelihood-diversification components (World Vision Iraq stories, Aug/Oct 2024).

## Activations
None confirmed. The Anticipation Hub records "1 activation" for this framework, but no
World Vision document gives a date, location, or outcome for it, and World Vision's own
lessons-learned report states the Iraq trigger simulation was mistimed and run in the
wrong scenario — a capacity gap, not a validated activation. The cash-for-seeds/fodder
distribution described in the fact sheet took place as a pilot activity, but whether it
followed an actual trigger firing is not stated in any source found. See
`extra.schema_strain`.
