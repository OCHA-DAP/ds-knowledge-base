# World Vision International — Lebanon flood

## Summary
World Vision Lebanon (WVL), as part of a regional World Vision Middle East and Eastern
Europe (WV MEER) anticipatory-action pilot covering Lebanon, Iraq and Syria, ran flood-focused
preparedness work in Akkar, northern Lebanon — an area prone to severe, frequent winter
flooding that particularly affects farmers and refugees living in Informal Tented
Settlements. The pilot was funded by Aktion Deutschland Hilft (ADH) from October 2023 to
December 2024; WVL separately secured World Vision Canada funding for a storm-water
drainage canal, rehabilitation of a flood-damaged school, and a remote early-warning-system
station aligned with Lebanon's national preparedness plan. Unlike the GloFAS/return-period
frameworks typical of this section, no quantitative forecast trigger has been published —
the program is better described as early-warning and mitigation infrastructure plus
recommendations for future trigger development.

## Trigger
No specific indicator/threshold/lead-time trigger is documented in public sources. The
fact sheet instead recommends (as forward-looking improvements, not an operating system):
community and municipal monitoring of soil saturation and river levels to strengthen early
warning, and closer collaboration with the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute (LARI),
the Ministry of Energy and Water, and the National Council for Scientific Research (CNRS) to
improve forecast reliability. The one concrete early-warning asset installed is a single
remote early-warning-system station in Akkar, funded by World Vision Canada and described as
aligned with Lebanon's national preparedness plan — no further technical detail (sensor
type, alert threshold) was found.

## Funding & scope
Two funding streams, neither broken out to a Lebanon-specific USD figure in public sources:
Aktion Deutschland Hilft funded the three-country (Lebanon/Iraq/Syria) WV MEER pilot from
October 2023 to December 2024; World Vision Canada funded Lebanon-specific infrastructure
(drainage canal, school rehabilitation, early-warning station). The Anticipation Hub
inventory records a target of 30,000 people and shows no populated funding figure
(`prearranged_funding_usd` left null — see `extra.funding_note`). Geographic focus is Akkar,
northern Lebanon.

## Activations
None confirmed. The fact sheet notes WVL has been "proactive in flood response, notably
during severe events in 2009, 2019 and 2024," but this describes the organisation's general
flood-response history, not a forecast-triggered anticipatory-action activation under this
program — no dated AA-specific activation was found in public sources.
