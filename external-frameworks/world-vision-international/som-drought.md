# World Vision International — Somalia drought

## Summary
The Anticipation Hub's global map lists an active World Vision International drought
(dry-spell) anticipatory action framework in Somalia, with a pre-arranged envelope of
USD 230,000 targeting 600 people — a small-scale framework relative to Somalia's other
listed drought frameworks (WFP: USD 8M/1.3M people; IFRC/SRCS: USD ~945K/30,000 people).
No World Vision-published framework document, trigger description, or activation record
was located despite searching World Vision's global and Somalia sites, the Anticipation
Hub's news and country pages, ReliefWeb, and Start Network materials. World Vision leads
the seven-NGO Somalia Resilience Programme (SomReP), which operates a community-based
early warning/early action system for drought — the most plausible institutional home for
this listing — but no source confirms that system is the specific framework being
described here (see `extra.coordination`).

## Trigger
Not publicly documented under World Vision's own name. SomReP's community-based early
warning/early action system (which World Vision co-leads among seven NGOs) trains
community Early Warning Committees to monitor local drought indicators and trigger
pre-agreed local actions, but no source ties specific thresholds or lead times to this
Hub-listed World Vision framework. See `extra.schema_strain`.

## Funding & scope
USD 230,000 pre-arranged, targeting 600 people, per the Anticipation Hub's Somalia
country-page listing (current as of this check). No funding source/donor breakdown, no
World Vision-published corroboration of either figure, and no coverage-area detail were
found.

## Activations
None known. No World Vision-attributed Somalia drought AA activation was found in the
sources reviewed.
