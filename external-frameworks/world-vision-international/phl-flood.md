# World Vision International — Philippines flood

## Summary
World Vision — through World Vision Development Foundation Inc. (WVDF), its Philippines
national office — has piloted a flood-focused anticipatory action approach in Pagalungan
Municipality, Maguindanao Province (Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao). The
pilot grew out of a wider World Vision Asia-Pacific regional AA project funded by Aktion
Deutschland Hilft (ADH) across six country offices (September 2022-December 2023). A
feasibility study (published ~February 2024) selected flood as the most feasible hazard
for AA in Pagalungan — over drought, disease, and armed conflict — but the design was
still incomplete at publication: trigger definition and a formal action protocol were
listed as next steps, not finished products. The Anticipation Hub's global map
nonetheless records a 2024 flood activation assisting roughly 5,000 people.

## Trigger
Not yet formalized in any public document. The feasibility study describes reliance on
PAGASA (the Philippine national weather agency) forecasts, which the study says typically
give about 3 days' warning, combined with a Pre-Disaster Risk Assessment (PDRA) and
activation of local Early Warning Systems; communities also use local water-level/flood
markers. No specific numeric threshold (e.g. a rainfall or river-level value) was found in
public sources — the study itself recommends further work to define the trigger and
finalize a protocol, so this should be read as a design-stage trigger rather than an
operating formula.

## Funding & scope
The Anticipation Hub inventory lists US$34,432 pre-arranged for this framework. The
Pagalungan pilot sits within World Vision's ADH-funded Asia-Pacific regional AA project
(Sept 2022-Dec 2023); the Hub does not disaggregate funding by source, so the exact share
of the $34,432 attributable to ADH versus other funding is unconfirmed. Geographic scope
is Pagalungan Municipality, seven priority barangays. The framework record itself lists 0
people targeted (consistent with a still-in-design pilot); the Hub's separate 2024
activation record lists ~5,000 people assisted using the same $34,432 figure — see
`extra.schema_strain`.

## Activations
One 2024 flood activation is recorded in the Anticipation Hub's global map for World
Vision International in the Philippines, assisting ~5,000 people at the same $34,432
figure as the pre-arranged funding. No independent news coverage or World Vision
publication describing this specific event (exact date, affected barangays, or which
trigger fired) was found — it is recorded here only as the Hub's aggregate figure shows
it, not independently confirmed.
