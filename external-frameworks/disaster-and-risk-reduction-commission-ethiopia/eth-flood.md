# Disaster and Risk Reduction Commission Ethiopia — Ethiopia flood

## Summary
The Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Commission's (EDRMC) "Simplified Flood Anticipatory
Action Framework for Kiremt 2025 – Ethiopia" (approved 12 June 2025), a government-owned,
government-issued framework developed with OCHA, the Ministry of Water and Energy (MoWE) and
the Ethiopian Meteorology Institute (EMI) as coordination co-leads. It is explicitly scoped as
a one-season instrument — "a simplified version to serve for the Kiremt 2025 season only" —
covering flood-prone woredas across Somali, Afar, Oromia, Amhara, Gambella, Sidama, Central
Ethiopia and South Ethiopia regions, targeting highly vulnerable households (with particular
attention to IDPs, refugees, the elderly, women, children and persons with disabilities) with
early actions spanning agriculture/livestock protection, evacuation, WASH, health/nutrition,
shelter, education-in-emergency and protection.

## Trigger
Two-stage. **Stage 1 – preparedness (~4 weeks ahead):** EMI's seasonal forecast shows ≥50%
probability of above-normal rainfall and EDRMC issues a national flood alert (for 2025,
Flood Alert #3 covering the Jun-Sep/JJAS Kiremt season); community preparedness and
prepositioning begin. **Stage 2 – activation (~7-10 days ahead):** high probability of
imminent flooding from short-term (10-day) rainfall forecasts and/or river-gauge data. The
document's example trigger combines a GloFAS river-discharge forecast at or above the
1-in-10-year return period with ≥75% probability within 7 days, CHIRPS/EMI rainfall forecasts
of ≥30mm across ≥50% of the target woreda/catchment, and MoWE discharge-based forecasting
against a flood threshold. For data-sparse areas the framework falls back to rainfall-analogue
years/historic flood events, or upland-rainfall estimates and dam-monitoring data for
downstream flood prediction. The AA Advisory Board validates the case for activation using
field data and technical verification before EDRMC issues the decision; the Board meets weekly
during high-risk periods.

## Funding & scope
The framework names government budget, CERF and local/international donors as potential
funding channels, plus pre-positioned in-kind stocks (WASH kits, food, seeds, fodder, NFIs),
but the public document states no committed USD amount or funding-source breakdown — once a
trigger is validated, the AA Advisory Board is to notify the relevant funder(s) and rapid
disbursement protocols are followed. The Anticipation Hub's country listing records an
associated USD 100,000 investment figure for this framework (kept as `prearranged_funding_usd`
here, sourced to the Hub rather than the framework document itself). No overall target-people
count is given; scope is "all highly vulnerable households" in the prioritized woredas listed
in `extra.target_areas`, with particular attention to IDPs, refugees, the elderly, women,
children and persons with disabilities.

## Activations
None confirmed in public reporting. EDRMC issued Flood Alert #3 for the Jun-Sep 2025 Kiremt
season shortly after the framework's approval, but no subsequent report of a validated Stage 2
trigger, cash disbursement or evacuation under this specific framework was found. The framework
was explicitly scoped to the 2025 Kiremt season only; no successor version was found in public
sources as of this check.
