# START — Bangladesh cold wave

## Summary
Start Fund Bangladesh — the 47-member national pooled fund modelled on the global Start
Fund — runs "Anticipatory alert B039" for cold waves in northern Bangladesh, developed with
FOREWARN Bangladesh from a six-year historical analysis of cold-wave events across 11
vulnerable districts. It was the first trigger-monitoring system FOREWARN built for a Start
Fund anticipatory alert, and was piloted outside the standard Start Fund alert cycle given
its different (forecast-triggered, tranched) design. Pre-arranges ~US$55,661 to reach around
1,000 people with cash and NFI support ahead of forecast cold waves.

## Trigger
FOREWARN Bangladesh delivers 7-day localised forecasts (from ECMWF and WRF models) to Start
Fund Bangladesh members. The alert activates in any of the 11 vulnerable districts when
forecast conditions meet any of three thresholds, each with a 7-day lead time: minimum
temperature below 8°C persisting at least two days; minimum temperature at or below 12°C
combined with a day/night range of 6°C or more; or minimum temperature at or below 6°C at a
specific location. Pre-arranged funds release in two tranches — readiness, then early
response — so early-response spending only happens once the alert is confirmed closer to
onset, protecting against a false readiness-stage trigger.

## Funding & scope
US$55,661 pre-arranged through Start Fund Bangladesh, targeting roughly 1,000 people (design
capacity per the Anticipation Hub map). The 2022 activation actually disbursed US$41,447 and
reached 5,981 people — see `extra.schema_strain` for the gap between the two figures. ESDO
(Eco-Social Development Organization) implements in Panchagarh and Nilphamari districts;
Humanity & Inclusion is listed by the Hub as an implementing partner but its specific role/
district is not confirmed in accessible sources.

## Activations
- **23-26 January 2022, Panchagarh & Nilphamari** — the alert's first and, per accessible
  sources, only activation to date. Threshold met in Panchagarh on 23 January and Nilphamari
  on 26 January; ESDO delivered BDT 2,450 (~EUR26) cash grants plus masks to 1,200 households
  within two days of notification. No cold-wave activation after 2022 was found.
