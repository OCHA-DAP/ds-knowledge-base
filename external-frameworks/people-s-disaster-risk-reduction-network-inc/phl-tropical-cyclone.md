# People's Disaster Risk Reduction Network, Inc. — Philippines tropical cyclone

## Summary
PDRRN is a Philippine NGO that implements anticipatory action (AA) for tropical cyclones
as a partner within two separate multi-org projects, rather than as author of its own
named framework. Since 2019 it has co-implemented **B-READY** — a forecast-based digital
cash-transfer pilot in Eastern Samar with Oxfam Pilipinas/Oxfam Novib, Plan International,
Global Parametrics and the PayMaya/Smart Padala digital-finance rails — and since 2024 it
has run an **Oxfam–PDRRN** AA model with the Virac local government in Catanduanes,
compared alongside FAO's and the Start Network's own AA models for the same cyclone
cluster in a 2025 Start Network learning review. Neither is part of the OCHA/CERF
collective Philippines framework (`frameworks/phl-storms`).

## Trigger
No single published PDRRN trigger document exists; the two known project triggers differ:
- **B-READY**: a proprietary Global Parametrics smart-data early-warning model forecasts
  storm track/wind impact and releases pre-arranged digital cash to registered households
  roughly 3 days ahead of landfall.
- **2024 Catanduanes (Oxfam–PDRRN/Virac LGU)**: a "soft trigger" based on projected
  rainfall and flood impact — a deliberately looser threshold than the windspeed-validated
  trigger FAO used in parallel in the same province for the same 2024 cyclones — releasing
  cash and enabling evacuation up to ~48 hours before landfall.

## Funding & scope
- **B-READY / Typhoon Odette, Dec 2021**: PHP 4,000,000, funded by the Dutch Relief
  Alliance, reaching ~2,650 families (40 barangays) in five Eastern Samar municipalities
  (Salcedo, Dolores, Lawaan, Quinapondan, Balangiga). The original B-READY pilot (2019)
  targeted ~2,000 households in seven coastal Salcedo communities.
- **2024 Catanduanes**: cash of US$56-60 per household; reported only as part of a
  combined Cagayan + Catanduanes total of ~21,250 households / ~US$205,300 — the
  Catanduanes/Oxfam-PDRRN-only slice is not broken out in sources found.
- The Anticipation Hub inventory (which generated this page) records a much smaller
  US$23,404 / 3,381 people for the "2024" PDRRN listing; this could not be matched to
  either activation above (see `extra.schema_strain`).

## Activations
- **Dec 2021 — Typhoon Rai/Odette**: B-READY's first documented activation (PHP 4M,
  ~2,650 families, Eastern Samar).
- **Oct-Nov 2024 — Typhoons Trami/Kristine and Man-yi/Pepito**: Oxfam–PDRRN's Catanduanes
  soft-trigger model activated alongside FAO's own AA model (Catanduanes) and the Start
  Ready Cagayan Consortium — documented as a three-model comparison in the Start
  Network's 2025 learning paper. No PDRRN-specific pre-2021 activation is confirmed in
  sources found.
