# WHH — Pakistan multiple

## Summary
Welthungerhilfe's (WHH) "Localized Multi-Hazard Anticipatory Action Facility Pakistan",
run with Alliance2015 partners (ACTED and Concern; the Anticipation Hub inventory records
ACTED and Muslim Aid as implementers), covers flood, heatwave and drought in Pakistan.
Per the Anticipation Hub, it targets some 483,000 people with ~US$86,580 pre-arranged.
Public material describes forecasting work with the Pakistan Meteorological Department
(PMD) but no single published trigger document.

## Trigger
WHH states it is developing "predictive models for hazards like heatwaves, floods, and
droughts" with PMD to forecast hazards and enable early, proactive intervention before
they strike. No numeric thresholds or lead times are published for this facility.

## Funding & scope
~US$86,580 pre-arranged (Anticipation Hub active-frameworks record) targeting ~483,000
people; no public per-donor breakdown for this specific facility (WHH's broader
anticipatory-action portfolio in Pakistan is described as government/donor co-funded,
including the German Federal Foreign Office). Geographic focus includes flood-prone
districts Lower Kohistan, Rajanpur, Naseerabad, Dadu and Sukkur, and heatwave-affected
Jacobabad and Bahawalpur, with attention to daily labourers and polio workers as
particularly vulnerable groups.

## Activations
- **2024, heatwave** — early action (prevention kits, cooling centres, heatstroke
  treatment) in Jacobabad and Bahawalpur; WHH reports up to an 85% reduction in
  heatstroke cases.
- **2024, flood** — early actions across five flood-prone districts.
- No specific activation dates, trigger values, or per-activation funding are published
  for this facility.
