# WHH — Pakistan flood

## Summary
Welthungerhilfe (WHH) finances a flood anticipatory-action project implemented by the
local NGO Fast Rural Development Program (FRDP) in flood-prone villages of Hyderabad,
Jamshoro and Dadu districts, Sindh province — "Protecting Lives Through Anticipatory
Action in Pakistan", rolled out from 5 September 2025. Per the Anticipation Hub's
inventory, the project pre-arranges roughly US$163,327 targeting some 7,000 people. It is
one of several distinct WHH-linked Pakistan instruments (see `extra.coordination` for how
it differs from WHH's own multi-hazard facility and from the Start Network DRF system
that WHH Pakistan also hosts).

## Trigger
No numeric trigger, gauge threshold, or lead time is published for this project. WHH
describes early-warning dissemination, safe-evacuation-route briefings, and evacuation
support (prioritising women, children, elderly and differently-abled residents) delivered
to flood-prone villages ahead of flood risk, plus IEC materials in local languages. WHH's
general global methodology for anticipatory action (used elsewhere in its portfolio, not
confirmed specific to this project) sets impact-based thresholds from historical-disaster
analysis — e.g. river water levels reaching "warning" level on a community-managed gauge —
with pre-arranged donor funding released once the threshold is met. A related research
study, "Hazard Risk and Impact Analysis... for Installation of Early Warning System... in
Dadu District" (University of Sindh, FRDP, WHH; EU DG-ECHO funded), suggests a formal
early-warning-system/trigger for Dadu was still under development as of 2025, not yet a
published operational threshold.

## Funding & scope
~US$163,327 pre-arranged, targeting ~7,000 people (Anticipation Hub inventory figures;
not independently corroborated in FRDP's or WHH's own project pages — see
`extra.schema_strain`). Geographic scope: flood-prone villages in Hyderabad, Jamshoro and
Dadu districts, Sindh province. Funder: Welthungerhilfe; a related early-warning-system
research study in Dadu was funded by the EU's DG-ECHO.

## Activations
- **5 September 2025** — FRDP, financed by WHH, rolled out early actions (risk-awareness
  dialogues, evacuation-route briefings, evacuation support for vulnerable groups, IEC
  materials) across flood-prone villages in Hyderabad, Jamshoro and Dadu. This fell within
  the wider catastrophic Aug-Sep 2025 Indus/Punjab flood period, during which the Pakistan
  Red Crescent/IFRC EAP and Start Network's Risk Pool 1 both also activated (see their
  pages), but the source describes this as a programme roll-out rather than stating a
  specific trigger breach.
- No earlier activation is documented in public sources reviewed; the Hub's 2024 listing
  for this record may reflect programme establishment rather than a dated activation.
