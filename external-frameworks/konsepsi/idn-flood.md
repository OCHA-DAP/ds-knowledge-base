# KONSEPSI — Indonesia flood (I CAN ACT)

## Summary
KONSEPSI NTB runs the **I CAN ACT** (Improvement Community Anticipatory Action) project,
a community-based flood anticipatory action programme in flood-prone villages of East and
West Lombok, West Nusa Tenggara. It is funded via Oxfam in Indonesia/Oxfam Australia
through the Australian NGO Cooperation Program (ANCP), backed by Australia's DFAT. The
project has run in three phases since August 2023 (Phase I: Sambelia District, East
Lombok; Phase II–III: expanded to West Lombok), combining forecast-based readiness
protocols with a pre-agreed Cash Transfer Programme (CTP) for registered vulnerable
households, including persons with disabilities. No real trigger activation has been
publicly confirmed to date.

## Trigger
Based on the Year 1 (Phase I) lesson-learned report: a **readiness** stage at roughly
D-10, triggered when a forecast shows >70% probability of cumulative rainfall exceeding
100mm over a 10-day period; and an **activation** stage (D-3 to D-1) triggered by any of
— a 3-day forecast of >100mm rainfall, 3 consecutive days of >100mm rainfall, >100mm
rainfall within 24 hours (confirmed against BMKG data or village ombrometers), or a
river-level rise of ≥75cm within one hour. The 100mm/day threshold was validated against
historical flood events specifically for Sambelia District. Activation releases the CTP
and evacuation support to pre-identified vulnerable households.

## Funding & scope
Anticipation Hub's global map records **USD 47,045** in pre-arranged funding targeting
**5,070 people**, flowing from DFAT → ANCP → Oxfam Australia/Oxfam in Indonesia →
KONSEPSI NTB (no public breakdown by phase or donor tranche). Phase I (Aug 2023–Jun 2024)
covered 3 villages in Sambelia District, East Lombok (Dara Kunci, Belanting, Obel-Obel);
Phase II (Jul 2024–Jun 2025) added Taman Ayu and Dasan Geria in West Lombok; Phase III
(Aug–Dec 2025) ran across all 4 villages and is marked completed on KONSEPSI's own site,
with no Phase IV announced. A separate Oxfam GB account describes cumulative reach of
"over 6,000 people" across five villages (see `extra.reach_discrepancy`).

## Activations
None confirmed. The Year 1 report explicitly states no trigger activation occurred during
Phase I. A 29 Feb 2024 table-top simulation exercise in Belanting (Sambelia) was a
scheduled readiness drill, not a confirmed-forecast response (see `extra.near_activation`).
A December 2025 dissemination event framed as covering "three years" of implementation
likewise made no mention of an actual activation.
