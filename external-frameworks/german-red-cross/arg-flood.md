# German Red Cross — Argentina flood

## Summary
A German Red Cross-coordinated flood anticipatory-action framework for the Argentine Red
Cross, first listed by the Anticipation Hub in 2021. Since 2019 the Argentine Red Cross
has been working to establish forecast-based financing (FbF) systems, and by April 2021 had
begun developing a flood Early Action Protocol (EAP) alongside a feasibility study on which
other hazards to cover. As of last_checked the framework remains in development: no trigger,
funding, target-people figure, or EAP document has been published.

## Trigger
Not yet defined in public sources. The April 2021 Anticipation Hub coverage of a Memorandum
of Understanding between Argentina's National Meteorological Service (Servicio
Meteorológico Nacional, SMN) and the Argentine Red Cross describes joint monitoring of
hydrometeorological phenomena, information-exchange channels, and impact-based forecasting
(IBF) methodologies as the building blocks for future anticipatory decision-making — but no
specific flood indicator, threshold, or lead time has been published. SMN became the first
national meteorological service worldwide to join the Anticipation Hub network as part of
this partnership.

## Funding & scope
