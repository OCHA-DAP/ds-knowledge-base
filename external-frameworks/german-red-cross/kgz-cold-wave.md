# German Red Cross — Kyrgyzstan cold wave EAP

## Summary
The Red Crescent Society of Kyrgyzstan's (RCSK) Early Action Protocol for cold waves
(EAP2024KG03, Operation MDRKG020), approved 13 August 2024. Funded through the IFRC DREF
Anticipatory Pillar, designed with technical support from German Red Cross (GRC) and the RCRC
Climate Centre. Covers 7 provinces of Kyrgyzstan and targets 10,000 vulnerable people (5,200
female / 4,800 male) with heaters, insulation, bedding and food parcels ahead of forecast cold
waves.

## Trigger
Kyrgyzhydromet, the national hydrometeorological service, is the sole official source of cold
wave forecasts/warnings in Kyrgyzstan; alerts are disseminated manually (email, fax, phone) to
RCSK under a pre-signed MoU. The RCSK president activates the EAP on receiving an alert, based
on forecasts giving roughly four days' lead time. A confirmation forecast at 72 hours before the
event acts as a "stop mechanism": if the modelled probability of the cold wave is below 75% at
that point, the RCSK Head of DM halts distribution, beneficiary verification and press releases
(a step costing ~6.5% of the total budget, ~CHF 30,868). If the later forecast reconfirms the
cold wave, early actions resume.

## Funding & scope
CHF 526,049 approved from the IFRC DREF Anticipatory Pillar (appeal MDR00001): CHF 413,165 for
readiness/pre-positioning + CHF 112,884 for early actions on trigger (≈US$597,104 per
Anticipation Hub reporting; exact conversion basis not stated). Targets 10,000 people (~2,000
most vulnerable families, selected via Ministry of Labor/Social Provision lists) across 7
provinces: Chuy, Naryn, Osh, Talas, Jalal-Abad, Batken and Issyk-Kul. Early actions: electrical
heaters, floor/window insulation film, blankets/mattresses/floor-mats, food parcels, and
awareness materials.

## Activations
None confirmed to date. The EAP was approved 13 August 2024, ahead of the December-February
cold-wave season, so winter 2024/25 was the first possible trigger window, but no activation
report has been located in public sources as of last_checked. A 2019-20 cold-wave response in
Naryn province (Jan 2020, ~1,000 people reached with coal/food/NFIs) pre-dates this EAP and was
explicitly a readiness drill/simulation rather than a real trigger-based activation.
