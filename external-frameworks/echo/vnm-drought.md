# ECHO — Viet Nam drought

## Summary
An anticipatory-action project for drought in Viet Nam, funded by EU Humanitarian Aid
(ECHO) and implemented by a consortium of CARE International in Viet Nam, World Vision,
and Plan International, with IMHEN (Institute of Meteorology, Hydrology and Environment)
as the national technical partner for drought monitoring and forecasting. The Anticipation
Hub inventory lists it under World Vision's implementation for 2023. Public documentation
is limited to a shared World Vision/CARE story rather than a formal framework document;
much of the operational detail (trigger specifics, scale, activation history) is not
publicly available (see `extra.schema_strain`).

## Trigger
IMHEN uses satellite-based drought monitoring together with climate/weather-research
forecasting modelling to inform the project's decision-making; cash assistance is
disbursed to at-risk households ahead of drought impact, letting recipients invest in
measures such as water storage tanks, medicine, and food supplies. No public source found
states the specific drought indicator, numeric threshold, or lead time used to decide when
to release funds.

## Funding & scope
The Anticipation Hub inventory records US$19,075 in pre-arranged funding and 626 target
people for this framework, attributed to ECHO funding and World Vision implementation.
These figures are small enough to plausibly reflect a single commune/pilot-scale slice of
the broader CARE/World Vision/Plan International consortium project rather than its full
budget or reach — no public source gives a total consortium-project budget, total
beneficiary count, or the full list of provinces covered. One implementation example named
in sources is "Thuan Hoa commune" (province not stated).

## Activations
None found in public reporting. Available sources describe general project activities and
outcomes (e.g. households using cash assistance for drought-preparedness purchases) rather
than a specific, dated trigger-fired activation.
