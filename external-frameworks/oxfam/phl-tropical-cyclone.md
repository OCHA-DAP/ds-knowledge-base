# Oxfam — Philippines tropical cyclone

## Summary
Oxfam Pilipinas' anticipatory action (AA) program for typhoons, run under the B-READY
banner since 2017 and expanded via SUPPA/SHARPER in 2019. Ahead of a forecast typhoon,
pre-arranged funds are released as unconditional cash to at-risk households — via
PayMaya/Smart Padala digital transfer — so families can buy supplies, secure boats,
harvest early and evacuate before landfall. No single published trigger threshold;
decisions draw on PAGASA data/forecasts, a Manila Observatory multi-hazard index, and
community-defined indicators. Listed by the Anticipation Hub since 2023. Distinct from,
but at times run alongside, the separate OCHA/CERF collective Philippines
tropical-cyclone framework, in which Oxfam is not an implementing agency.

## Trigger
No quantified wind-speed or rainfall threshold is stated in any public Oxfam source.
The program combines: PAGASA historical typhoon data and forecasts from 20+ agencies;
a multi-hazard (flood/drought/typhoon) index co-developed with the Manila Observatory
to set thresholds; and community-defined indicators (e.g. fisherfolk-observed early
storm-surge signs, farmer-set rainfall thresholds), reflecting B-READY's
participatory design. In earlier iterations (2021 Eastern Samar), the SHARPER/B-READY
combination used Global Parametrics' early-warning technology. Cash is released days
before landfall — reported activations describe distributions roughly 1–3 days ahead,
with households acting (evacuating, securing assets) up to 48 hours before impact.

## Funding & scope
The Anticipation Hub's global-map inventory (the only source with a single
programme-level figure) lists ~US$170,000 pre-arranged for ~4,000 people — but this
figure is not corroborated elsewhere and looks much smaller than what individual
recent activations have actually moved (see Activations, and `extra.schema_strain`).
No consistent funding source recurs across activations: named funders include Oxfam
Novib, Oxfam America, the Dutch Relief Alliance (2021), and — per Oxfam's own account
of Philippines AA funding generally — Oxfam, FAO, Start Network and CERF, without a
disclosed per-activation split. Geographic coverage has included Eastern Samar,
Cotabato City (SUPPA, floods), Catanduanes and Cagayan; BARMM is a current focus of the
Manila Observatory multi-hazard index work.

## Activations
- **~13 Dec 2021 — Typhoon Odette (Rai)**: ~PHP 4 million pre-disaster cash to 2,650
  families across 40 barangays in five Eastern Samar towns (Salcedo, Dolores, Lawaan,
  Quinapondan, Balangiga), ~3 days ahead of landfall; Dutch Relief Alliance-funded,
  delivered with Oxfam Novib/America, PDRRN, Plan International and Global Parametrics.
- **Nov 2024 — Typhoons Trami and Man-yi (Pepito)**: ~21,250 households in Cagayan and
  Catanduanes received US$56–60 each (~US$205,300 total) to prepare and evacuate up to
  48 hours ahead of landfall.
- **2025 — Typhoons Opong, Tino and Uwan (Fung-Wong)**: three further activations;
  results reviewed jointly with Start Network and the Office of Civil Defense
  (reported April 2026). Per-activation figures not published. The Uwan/Fung-Wong
  activation ran alongside the separate OCHA/CERF collective framework's own
  activation for the same storm.
