# Oxfam — Timor-Leste flood

## Summary
Oxfam is one of five international NGOs (alongside World Vision, CARE International,
CARITAS and Plan International) piloting flood Anticipatory Action in Timor-Leste under
the Australian-government-funded Disaster READY project, through the Australian
Humanitarian Partnership's (AHP) Anticipatory Action Fund (AAF). Oxfam's slice of the
pilot targets two villages in Oecusse municipality. It is not an independently designed
Oxfam framework — see `extra.coordination` for how the five INGO pieces divide the
country and how the Anticipation Hub attributes the aggregate mechanism.

## Trigger
The AAF releases pre-arranged funds and community-level anticipatory actions against
pre-agreed triggers built on weather forecasts, but no public document specifies the
indicator, threshold or lead time for the Oecusse flood pilot specifically. In February
2025 a readiness activation was triggered in Oecusse by flood forecasts; the fund's full
activation threshold was not met, so no AA funds were released, but the readiness stage
generated learning for the mechanism (see `extra.near_activation`).

## Funding & scope
Anticipation Hub inventory: $19,789 (AHP-funded) targeting 631 people in Oxfam's two
Oecusse villages — one small piece of the AHP's AUD 3.315 million Anticipatory Action Fund
trial across Timor-Leste, Vanuatu and Papua New Guinea (through December 2026).

## Activations
None confirmed as full Anticipatory Action activations as of `last_checked`. A February
2025 readiness activation in Oecusse (forecast-triggered, threshold for full AA not met)
is recorded in `extra.near_activation` rather than here, per the "trigger-not-met"
activation flavor.
