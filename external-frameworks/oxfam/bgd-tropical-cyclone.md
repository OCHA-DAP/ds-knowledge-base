# Oxfam — Bangladesh tropical cyclone

## Summary
Oxfam in Bangladesh runs a "Cyclone Anticipatory Action Planning and Response" project
with local partner Jago Nari, piloted in the coastal district of Barguna (Jago Nari's
home base since 1998). The project sits inside Jago Nari's broader Shelter-Centric
Anticipatory Action (SCAA) model — a 2025 institutional framework that turns cyclone
shelters into hubs for early warning, evacuation, anticipatory cash, health/protection
services and post-event recovery, aligned with the Government of Bangladesh's Standing
Orders on Disaster and National Early Action Protocol (NEAP) for cyclones. Jago Nari also
operates related, separately-funded AA activity through Start Network's Start Ready
mechanism and CARE Bangladesh's CARE Alert project (see Coordination) — this page covers
only the Oxfam-funded line, per the Anticipation Hub's own record-keeping.

## Trigger
The SCAA model's four-stage sequence: **Readiness** (routine training/drills/stock
checks); **Early Alert** (>72–48 hours before landfall — forecast verification, volunteer
standby, shelter preparation and prepositioning); **Trigger Activation** (72–36 hours
before landfall — shelter opening, evacuation, and mobilisation of anticipatory
cash/food/health/water services); **Post-Impact** (0–72 hours after landfall — continued
service delivery, transition to emergency response). Forecasts come from the Bangladesh
Meteorological Department (BMD); the go/no-go reference is the Government's NEAP trigger
levels for cyclones, rather than a threshold Jago Nari or Oxfam sets independently. Cash
assistance is an "optional module": pre-identified vulnerable households receive
unconditional, mobile-money cash on NEAP trigger confirmation. No public document states
a numeric wind-speed or BMD-signal threshold specific to this Oxfam-funded project (see
`extra.schema_strain`).

## Funding & scope
The Anticipation Hub's inventory records this framework (org: Oxfam; implementing:
Oxfam, Jago Nari) at US$93,668 pre-arranged, targeting 2,606 people — figures that could
not be independently corroborated elsewhere and are carried forward from the Hub as the
only public numbers found. No funding-source breakdown or validity period is stated
publicly. Geographic scope is Barguna district, with related activity (funded
separately, via Start Ready/CARE Alert rather than Oxfam) also reaching Patuakhali and
Bhola.

## Activations
- **26 May 2024, Cyclone Remal** — Oxfam's Cyclone Anticipatory Action Planning and
  Response project disbursed anticipatory cash grants to over 600 households ahead of
  landfall, as part of Oxfam's wider relief response across Patuakhali, Barguna and Cox's
  Bazar.
- An earlier, reactive (not forecast-triggered) Oxfam-funded Jago Nari cash response to
  Cyclone Fani (May 2019) is documented but predates the current anticipatory-action
  project design; not counted as a formal AA activation here.
