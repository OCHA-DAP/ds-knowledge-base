# Oxfam — Philippines El Niño

## Summary
Oxfam Pilipinas leads SUPREME BARMM, an EU (ECHO)-funded, multi-agency consortium
supporting the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) regional
government's early-warning and anticipatory-action work. With BARMM's READi programme and
its Pre-Disaster Risk Assessment (PDRA) Group, Oxfam Pilipinas helped develop AA triggers
and early-action protocols for tropical cyclones, flooding and El Niño. In February 2024 the
El Niño/drought protocol produced the Philippines' first reported anticipatory-action
drought activation, delivering water-conservation kits, hygiene kits and vegetable seeds to
2,000 families in BARMM's Special Geographic Areas.

## Trigger
A level-one drought activation, part of a broader (undisclosed-threshold) protocol Oxfam
Pilipinas built with BARMM READi/PDRA covering tropical cyclones, flooding and El Niño.
Public reporting describes the *inputs* — PAGASA and Manila Observatory forecasts flagging
BARMM's Special Geographic Area as high-risk and the region as already at "medium" drought
risk — rather than a stated numerical threshold or lead time. Level-one early actions:
household awareness-raising sessions and distribution of rainwater harvesters, water
storage containers, hygiene kits and vegetable seeds, aimed at helping communities cope with
the initial impacts of an El Niño dry spell.

## Funding & scope
Funded through the EU's Civil Protection and Humanitarian Aid department (ECHO) as part of
the SUPREME BARMM project; no public source states a specific pre-arranged funding figure
for the El Niño/drought component. ~2,000 families targeted in the February 2024 activation,
in BARMM's Special Geographic Areas (including the Pikit Cluster and coastal barangays such
as Barungis). Consortium partners: ACCORD Incorporated, Action Against Hunger Philippines,
CARE Philippines, Community Organizers Multiversity, Humanity & Inclusion Philippines,
Manila Observatory, PDRRN, Plan International Philippines, alongside BARMM's own READi/PDRA
structures. Not connected to OCHA/CERF's Philippines framework, which is tropical-cyclone
only (see `extra.coordination`).

## Activations
- **5 Feb 2024 (reported)** — Level-one drought trigger met; SUPREME BARMM, led by Oxfam
  Pilipinas, distributed water conservation kits (rainwater harvesters, water storage
  containers), hygiene kits and vegetable seeds to 2,000 families across BARMM's Special
  Geographic Area ahead of the 2024 El Niño dry spell — described by Oxfam as the
  Philippines' first anticipatory action activation for drought.

No earlier or later El Niño/drought activations were found in public sources; a 2026
"Strong El Niño" briefing (under the newer ACCESS consortium, see `extra.successor_context`)
indicates continued readiness work but no activation reported as of this check.
