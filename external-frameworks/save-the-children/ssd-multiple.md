# Save the Children — South Sudan multiple

## Summary
Save the Children (through Save the Children Denmark and Save the Children South
Sudan) runs a locally-led, child-centred anticipatory action pilot in Maban County,
Upper Nile State — an area hosting over 170,000 refugees and hit by recurring flash
flooding since 2019. Rather than a calibrated forecast-trigger EAP, the programme
builds on existing education and child-protection work: child-led Disaster Risk
Reduction (DRR) Clubs in around ten schools carry out peer education on flood
early-warning signs and use small grants for protective measures (trenches, fences,
window repairs, food-security gardens) ahead of the flood season. It is funded by
DANIDA (Danish International Development Agency).

## Trigger
No public document defines a calibrated indicator/threshold trigger. The informal
early-warning basis reported in project write-ups is that flash floods in Maban
typically follow rainfall in the Ethiopian highlands upstream by around **two days** —
DRR Club members disseminate this warning peer-to-peer so schools and communities can
take pre-agreed, no-regret protective actions (safeguarding school infrastructure and
materials, using trenches/fences to hold back floodwater) before water arrives. This is
a community/school-based early-warning-to-action process, not a single quantitative
threshold releasing pre-arranged funding.

## Funding & scope
Funded by DANIDA; no public USD/DKK figure specific to the anticipatory-action
component was found. Scale documented publicly: DRR Clubs and protective works in
~10 schools across Maban County. Maban hosts 171,162 refugees (per the 2025 case
study) — cited as county context, not a stated AA target-population figure.

## Activations
No discrete, dated "trigger fired" activation events were found in public reporting —
this reads as an ongoing, recurring community/school-level DRR-AA programme (annual
flood seasons since roughly 2021-2023) rather than a framework with individually
dated activation records. "None known" in the `activations` field reflects that.
