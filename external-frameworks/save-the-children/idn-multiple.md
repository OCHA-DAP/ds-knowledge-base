# Save the Children — Indonesia multiple hazards (drought, flood, landslide)

## Summary
Save the Children Indonesia runs a multi-hazard anticipatory action (AA) plan covering
drought, flood, and landslide, implemented with the Indonesian Red Cross Society (PMI),
Indonesian government agencies (BNPB, BMKG, PVMBG), OCHA, IFRC, and several of Save the
Children's own local NGO partners (Yayasan Geutanyoe, IDEP, CIS Timor, NFDMC, Yayasan
IBU, Sulawesi Cipta Forum). It targets 15,000 people and was activated for drought in
2024, reaching 7,000 people with USD 45,000 in funding. It sits within Save the
Children's global AA programme (23 Early Action Protocols/Anticipatory Action Plans
across 15 countries as of end-2024, guided by the organisation's global "Framework for
Anticipatory Action") rather than as an Indonesia-specific published protocol.

## Trigger
No public document specifies Save the Children's own numeric threshold, indicator, or
lead time for Indonesia. Indonesia's national AA/disaster-alert system generally works
by converting early warnings from BMKG (meteorological/climatological/geophysical
agency) and PVMBG (volcanological and geological hazard mitigation agency) into a
government-declared **Siaga Darurat** ("Emergency Alert"), issued by BNPB or local
disaster agencies (BPBD) under Presidential Regulations No. 21–22/2008 and BNPB
Regulation No. 4/2022 — this is the mechanism used elsewhere in the country for drought
AA (e.g. the NTT Governor's "Imminent Disaster Declaration for Drought" in both 2023 and
2024). Save the Children's 2024 Indonesia activation was for drought and is described as
based on "pre-identified triggers," but no threshold or forecast product is public. See
`extra.schema_strain`.

## Funding & scope
**USD 45,000** funded the 2024 drought activation, targeting up to 15,000 people
framework-wide and reaching **7,000 people** in the 2024 activation. The funding source
breakdown for Indonesia specifically is not public; Save the Children's global 2024 AA
programme (USD 2.99 million across 11 activating countries) drew on a pooled set of
donors including Save the Children International's Humanitarian Fund, the German
Federal Foreign Office, DANIDA, UNOCHA, Start Network, Norad, and others (see
`extra.funding_note`).

## Activations
- **2024 (drought)** — USD 45,000, reaching 7,000 people. Partners: OCHA, IFRC,
  Indonesian Red Cross Society (PMI), BNPB, BMKG, PVMBG, and Save the Children's local
  implementing partners. No other activations are publicly documented; "none known"
  applies to years before 2024.
