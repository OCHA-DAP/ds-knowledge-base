# Save the Children — Nepal flood

## Summary
Save the Children Nepal Country Office runs a Flood Early Action Protocol (EAP), dated
2023, for Raptisonari and Narainapur rural municipalities in Banke district, western
Nepal, along the West Rapti river. It was developed with local and national authorities,
the Social Development Forum Banke, the Nepal Red Cross Society, and technical support
from the Red Cross Climate Centre. On activation, cash assistance or value vouchers go to
pre-registered at-risk households; the Anticipation Hub inventory lists it as active for
both the 2023 and 2024 monsoon seasons, targeting roughly 3,000 people with US$43,000
(or, per a separate Hub table, US$88,000 — unreconciled, see `extra.funding_note`)
pre-arranged. No confirmed activation has been found in public sources.

## Trigger
Two-stage, percentile/probability-based forecast trigger evaluated for the West Rapti
basin at the Kusum or Bagasoti monitoring points:
- **Readiness:** weather monitoring shows ≥50% probability of rainfall over the West
  Rapti basin within the next 3-5 days.
- **Action:** weather monitoring shows extreme rainfall (>95th percentile) over the same
  basin/watershed within 3-5 days.

On the action trigger firing, cash assistance/value vouchers are released to households
pre-registered (by June each year) against local disaster-management-committee and social
security data. Beneficiary numbers are set against a larger surveyed at-risk population —
see `extra.at_risk_households`.

## Funding & scope
US$43,000 pre-arranged for ~3,000 people, per the Anticipation Hub global-map inventory;
a separate Hub country-page table gives US$88,000 for the same nominal framework — the
two figures are not reconciled in public sources (`extra.funding_note`). Funding source
(Save the Children's own funds vs. an external donor) is not stated. Implementation runs
through the rural municipalities' disaster management funds/budgets, coordinated with
Save the Children, Social Development Forum Banke, and the Nepal Red Cross Society.

## Activations
None confirmed in public sources for the 2023 or 2024 monsoon seasons. Save the
Children's September 2024 flood response in Nepal (tarpaulins, blankets, water filters,
mosquito nets, hygiene kits) is documented as a post-event humanitarian response, not
explicitly tied to this EAP's pre-arranged trigger — see `extra.schema_strain`.
