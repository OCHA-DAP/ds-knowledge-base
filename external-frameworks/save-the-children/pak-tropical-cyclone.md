# Save the Children — Pakistan tropical cyclone

## Summary
The Anticipation Hub's global map lists a 2024 "Cyclone / Typhoon / Hurricane" framework
for Pakistan coordinated and implemented by Save the Children. No public evidence of a
Pakistan- or cyclone-specific trigger, EAP, SOP, funding envelope, or activation was found;
the Hub's own record for this entry shows zero target people and zero investment, and its
linked document is Save the Children's global Children's Emergency Fund annual report
rather than a country/hazard-specific plan (see `extra.schema_strain`).

## Trigger
Not found. No public Save the Children document defines a Pakistan cyclone
indicator/threshold/lead time. Save the Children's org-wide 2022 "Framework for
Anticipatory Action" sets general principles for developing Early Action Protocols but
contains no country- or hazard-specific thresholds.

## Funding & scope
Not found as a standalone figure. The Hub's active-frameworks record for this entry shows
`people_targeted: 0` and `investment_usd: 0`. Save the Children's Children's Emergency Fund
(the document linked from the Hub record) put $6.9M — about 16% of its 2024 spend — toward
anticipatory action and preparedness across 47 countries globally, but does not break this
out by country or hazard for Pakistan cyclones specifically.

## Activations
None known. No news or Hub record of a Save the Children cyclone anticipatory-action
activation in Pakistan was found (including around Cyclone Biparjoy, June 2023, the most
recent cyclone to threaten Pakistan's coast).
