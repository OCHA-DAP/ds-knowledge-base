# Save the Children — Nigeria multiple

## Summary
Save the Children runs its own anticipatory action (AA) activity in Nigeria across
multiple hazards, operating under its 2022 global *Framework for Anticipatory Action*
and financed chiefly through its own Children's Emergency Fund (CEF) rather than a
country-specific pre-arranged envelope. Its most documented 2024 Nigeria activity was
cash and voucher assistance (CVA) delivered ahead of and during that year's flood
season, compared against shock-responsive cash in a case-study evaluation, plus
early-warning messaging and community cash-for-work. It is separate from, and not an
implementing partner in, the OCHA/CERF-coordinated Nigeria flood collective framework
(FAO/WFP/UNICEF/NRC/CRUDAN).

## Trigger
No numeric trigger indicator, threshold, or lead time is stated in any public document
found. Save the Children's global AA framework (2022) sets organisational principles
and process rather than a country trigger protocol. Its 2024 Nigeria flood CVA
programme, described in *A Journey to Resilience: Cash for Anticipatory Action in
Nigeria* (2025), compared anticipatory (pre-flood) cash against shock-responsive
(post-flood) cash, alongside early-warning messaging and cash-for-work — but the
specific forecast product, threshold, or lead time used to decide when to disburse the
anticipatory cash is not stated in the sources accessible to this research (the report's
PDF text could not be extracted here). See `extra.schema_strain`.

## Funding & scope
No standing pre-arranged funding envelope or target population is publicly recorded —
the Anticipation Hub's Nigeria country-page inventory lists $0 / 0 people for the
standing framework row. Funding instead flows case-by-case from Save the Children's
global CEF, which allocated USD 6.9 million (16% of the Fund) to anticipatory action and
preparedness across 47 countries in 2024. For Nigeria specifically, the Hub inventory
records one 2024 activation totalling USD 128,000 reaching approximately 3,000 people;
state/LGA-level scope is not stated in the sources found.

## Activations
- **2024 (flood season)** — anticipatory CVA released ahead of/during Nigeria's 2024
  flood season, per the Anticipation Hub Nigeria country-page inventory: USD 128,000
  reaching ~3,000 people. Documented narratively (without a confirmed dollar or
  beneficiary figure in the material accessible here) in *A Journey to Resilience*
  (Save the Children, 2025), which evaluates this anticipatory CVA against a
  shock-responsive comparison group. Exact activation date and geographic breakdown are
  not stated in the public sources found.
- **Not counted as an activation:** a separate USD 300,000 CEF disbursement responded to
  worsening conflict-driven food insecurity in northern Nigeria in 2024 (per the CEF 2024
  annual impact report) — this reads as a response to an already-deteriorating situation,
  not a pre-agreed anticipatory trigger, so it is not listed as an AA activation here.
