# Save the Children — Somalia multiple

## Summary
Unlike the single-EAP-style pages elsewhere in this KB, Save the Children does not appear
to publish one standalone, quantified anticipatory-action (AA) trigger document for
Somalia. What is publicly documented is a cluster of related activities: the
organisation's global 2022 "Framework for Anticipatory Action" (org-wide policy, not
Somalia-specific); Household Economy Analysis (HEA)-based AA research and piloting in
Somalia and Kenya as a founding member of the Jameel Observatory for Food Security Early
Action; a German Federal Foreign Office-funded research project mapping early-warning and
AA gaps for drought and flood across Somalia (six workshops with government, NGOs and
pastoralists; research agenda finalised and report published 9 June 2025); and a
early-warning-systems partnership with Somalia's Disaster Management Authority (SoDMA),
reported in April 2024. Separately, Save the Children is one of several NGOs delivering
cash transfers under Start Network's own ARC Replica parametric drought framework in
Somalia (see `extra.coordination`) — that is a distinct, separately-recorded framework, not
this one.

## Trigger
No numeric threshold, lead time, or automatic-release mechanism was found in public
sources for an SC-owned Somalia framework. The general approach described in Save the
Children/Jameel Observatory material is HEA-based: analysing household-level economic and
food-security data — including seasonal Gu (March-June) and Deyr (October-December)
rainfall performance — to project when and where households will need assistance, feeding
into anticipatory programming across multiple hazards (hence the Hub's "multiple" hazard
tag) rather than a single hazard-specific trigger. The GFFO-funded research project's own
finding was that "numerous challenges" limit early-warning uptake in Somalia; its output
was a research and learning agenda, not an operational trigger protocol.

## Funding & scope
No prearranged funding total or target-people figure is stated in the Anticipation Hub's
own record for this framework, and none was found in independent public sources for an
SC-owned instrument. The GFFO funds the Somalia research/learning-agenda project; no
public figure for that grant was found. Save the Children separately shares in Start
Network's ARC Replica drought payouts as an implementing partner (e.g. part of the
2023 combined ~US$4.2M payout reaching 51,318 people) — that funding is Start Network's,
recorded on its own page, not attributed here (see `extra.coordination`).

## Activations
None known for an SC-owned/branded Somalia AA framework. Save the Children's participation
in Start Network's Somalia drought ARC Replica activations (2023, 2025, 2026) is recorded
on [`external-frameworks/start-network/som-drought.md`](../start-network/som-drought.md),
not here.
