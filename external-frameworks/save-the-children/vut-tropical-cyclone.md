# Save the Children — Vanuatu tropical cyclone

## Summary
Save the Children's anticipatory action (AA) program for tropical cyclones in Vanuatu,
listed as an active framework by the Anticipation Hub since 2024 (US$137,010
pre-arranged). It grew out of 2023 donor funding to trial AA for cyclones in Vanuatu and,
in parallel, floods in Papua New Guinea: a 2023 consultancy was tasked with building
readiness and activation triggers into an AA "tool" (referred to in the consultancy's
Terms of Reference as the "Yumi Redi early warning tool"), working with in-country INGOs
and the government. No public document sets out an endorsed trigger, and no activation
has been recorded.

## Trigger
No public source states a specific indicator, threshold, or lead time. Per the 2023
consultancy Terms of Reference, the design process was to incorporate both **readiness
triggers** and **activation triggers** into an AA tool, based on a review of roughly the
last 30 years of historical weather data for cyclonic disasters plus national, regional
and global forecast sources, with a functional risk-monitoring system and defined
decision-making roles. The tool was to be socialised with government, NGO and civil
society actors, and to help Start Network members raise anticipatory alerts. Whether
this became — or is distinct from — the long-running "Yumi Redi" multi-agency (Save the
Children, Red Cross, CARE, Oxfam) cyclone-preparedness campaign (documented since
2016/17) is not clarified in public sources.

## Funding & scope
US$137,010 pre-arranged per the Anticipation Hub inventory (fetched 2026-07-10, still
current as of 2026-08-02); no donor/source breakdown, and no target-people figure, is
disclosed in public sources. The 2023 consultancy funding that seeded this work is not
itself confirmed to be the same pre-arranged sum.

## Activations
None known. The Anticipation Hub's Vanuatu country page reports 0 activations for this
framework as of last check.
