# Save the Children — Mozambique drought

## Summary
Save the Children's Mozambique "drought" entry in the Anticipation Hub inventory refers
to **LINK** (GCF project SAP042), a Green Climate Fund-financed climate-resilience and
social-protection programme running November 2024-June 2030 across nine districts in
Gaza, Manica and Tete provinces. Rather than a narrow single-hazard early action
protocol, it is a broader US$28.3M programme — accredited through Save the Children
Australia — that integrates climate-adaptation planning into the government's
Productive Social Action Programme (PASP) cash-transfer system and includes
strengthened drought early-warning and anticipatory-action capacity as one component
alongside drought-tolerant food value chains and disaster-risk reduction.

## Trigger
No single quantitative trigger (indicator, threshold, lead time) has been published.
Save the Children and the GCF describe LINK as embedding early-warning systems and
"pre-defined drought triggers" into social-protection targeting so that PASP cash
transfers can be informed by risk information ahead of a shock, but no public source
tabulates a specific numeric threshold, forecast product, or lead time for this
programme specifically (contrast with WFP's own, separately-run Mozambique drought
framework, which does publish a detailed SPI/ECMWF-based trigger menu — see
`external-frameworks/wfp/moz-drought.md`).

## Funding & scope
Total project cost US$28.3M, of which the GCF grant is US$23.5M; remaining co-financing
(US$3.45M in-kind + US$1.38M grants) is not itemized by source on the GCF's own project
page, though Save the Children's announcement names Save the Children Italy, Norad, the
Government of Mozambique and AEROMAP as co-financing partners. Approved by the GCF Board
on 18 July 2024 (reportedly the first social-protection project the GCF has approved in
Mozambique). Targets 414,857 direct beneficiaries (plus 559,863 indirect, per Swiss Re
Foundation) across nine districts in Gaza, Manica and Tete provinces.

## Activations
None known/applicable — LINK is a multi-year programme rollout (2024-2030) rather than
a discrete-trigger EAP that activates on a per-event basis. The Hub's single "2024"
listing corresponds to the GCF Board's July 2024 approval, not an activation event.
