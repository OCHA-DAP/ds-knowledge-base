# Action against hunger — Bangladesh tropical cyclone

## Summary
Action Against Hunger (ACF) runs ECHO-funded anticipatory cash assistance for
cyclone-prone coastal communities in Bangladesh, coordinating with local partners
including Uttaran, South Asia Partnership, and Sheba Manab Kallyan Kendra (per the
Anticipation Hub listing). No formal public EAP/protocol document was found; the one
recorded activation (Cyclone Remal, May 2024) moved cash to at-risk households in
Satkhira district on a Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) cyclone danger signal.
Separately, ACF is piloting SURF-IT, an AI-based storm/tidal-surge forecasting system
with Uttaran and North South University, still in development. This program runs
independently of the OCHA/CERF collective Bangladesh cyclone framework (see Coordination).

## Trigger
No formal public trigger document/SOP with specific thresholds or lead times was found.
The one recorded activation moved on a general BMD cyclone danger signal (Signal 10 for
Remal) rather than a documented ACF-specific threshold. The SURF-IT pilot is developing
an AI/LSTM surge-forecasting model intended to give 2-3 days' advance warning via SMS, but
it remains in data-collection/design phase and is not yet an operational go/no-go trigger
(see `extra.surf_it`).

## Funding & scope
No stated pre-arranged funding total or forward target-people figure was found (see
`extra.schema_strain`). The 2024 Remal activation was funded by the European Union
(ECHO) and delivered ~€38 (~US$40) per person to over 2,000 people in Satkhira district.
The SURF-IT forecasting pilot is funded ~90% by UK FCDO, co-funded by Canada's IDRC,
via the CLARE programme.

## Coordination
ACF is not one of the four CERF-funded UN implementing agencies (FAO, UNFPA, UNICEF, WFP)
in the OCHA/CERF collective Bangladesh cyclone framework (`frameworks/bgd-cyclone`) and
was not found named in that framework's document. ACF's cash response and the SURF-IT
pilot appear to run independently of that collective framework.

## Activations
- **26 May 2024 — Cyclone Remal**: BMD Danger Signal 10. ECHO-funded ACF anticipatory
  cash (~€38/US$40 per person) reached over 2,000 vulnerable people in Satkhira district
  ahead of landfall, enabling purchases such as rope, dried food, and fishpond netting;
  one farmer reported avoiding over €600 in damages. No other activations found on record.
