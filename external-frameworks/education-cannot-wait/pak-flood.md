# Education Cannot Wait — Pakistan flood

## Summary
A US$2.5 million, 18-month anticipatory-action pilot funded by Education Cannot Wait
(ECW) and delivered by UNICEF with Save the Children and the Punjab Education
Department, launched 23 September 2024 across six flood-prone districts in Punjab
(Muzaffargarh, Rajanpur) and Sindh (Sukkur, Larkana, Dadu, Shaheed Benazirabad). It
pre-finances education-sector actions — protecting school assets, standing up temporary
learning spaces, catch-up learning plans — so that riverine flood forecasts trigger
action before schools are damaged or lessons are lost, rather than only funding recovery
afterwards.

## Trigger
Government flood forecasts and river-gauge data set two thresholds for schools in the
pilot districts: a **"ready"** stage and an **"activate"** stage. Once the activate
threshold is met, pre-agreed actions are carried out within an estimated 48-72 hours:
moving and securing school assets and records, shifting to temporary learning spaces or
remote modalities, and preparing catch-up learning plans. Specific numeric gauge or
forecast thresholds, and the named hydromet product behind them, are not published in
the sources reviewed.

## Funding & scope
US$2.5 million from ECW (part of a joint US$5M Pakistan+Somalia package), delivered by
UNICEF in coordination with federal/provincial disaster-management authorities, hydromet
agencies, and provincial/district education departments; Save the Children is a named
implementing partner in Punjab. Six target districts across Punjab and Sindh (see
`extra.districts`); the Anticipation Hub inventory lists a $750,000 / 30,000-person
figure that could not be reconciled with ECW's own $2.5M announcement (see
`extra.funding_note`).

## Activations
- **August 2025, Punjab (Muzaffargarh, Rajanpur)** — the pilot's one documented
  activation: flood alerts triggered evacuation of 62 schools, protecting learning
  materials for 12,500+ students (7,000+ girls), and Temporary Learning Spaces in 37
  schools kept 6,000+ children learning without a lost day. No activation figures for
  the four Sindh districts were found. A separate, non-AA emergency-response grant
  announced in Nov 2025 (`extra.related_response`) confirms the pilot was still live
  through at least that period.
