# Australian Humanitarian Partnership Support Unit — Timor-Leste flood

## Summary
The Australian Humanitarian Partnership (AHP) Anticipatory Action Fund (AAF) is a
small-scale, donor-funded trial — AUD 3.315 million from the Australian Government across
Timor-Leste, Vanuatu, Papua New Guinea and Solomon Islands — that pre-positions funding for
community-based anticipatory action ahead of forecast hazards. In Timor-Leste it builds on
existing Disaster READY Community-Based Disaster Risk Management (CBDRM) relationships and
is implemented through four partners: Church Agencies Network – Disaster Operation (CAN DO,
led by Caritas Australia), CARE International, Oxfam and World Vision International. The
Anticipation Hub records the Timor-Leste flood component at 5,053 people targeted and USD
125,015 in pre-arranged funding.

## Trigger
AHP describes the AAF mechanism only at a programmatic level: activities and fund
disbursement are triggered by a "pre-agreed trigger — an indicator that a hazard is imminent
and severe," derived from risk analysis and forecasts, following a "no-regrets" philosophy
(a trigger that fires without a subsequent disaster is still valued for the preparedness and
learning it generates). No public source specifies the exact rainfall/river indicator,
numeric threshold, or lead time used for the Timor-Leste flood framework specifically. In
February 2025 a flood forecast for Oecusse municipality triggered the fund's readiness
stage, but the separate threshold for full AA release was not reached.

## Funding & scope
AUD 3.315 million total, committed by the Australian Government (DFAT) via the AHP Support
Unit, to trial anticipatory action across all four Phase 1–3 countries through December
2026 — this is not a Timor-Leste-only figure. The Anticipation Hub's Timor-Leste-specific
record shows USD 125,015 pre-arranged funding targeting 5,053 people, delivered through the
four implementing partners above. The AAF launched via expression of interest in early 2023,
with Timor-Leste and Vanuatu selected for the first trial phase.

## Activations
No full trigger-met activation is confirmed in the sources reviewed. A February 2025
readiness-stage activation in Oecusse (flood forecast) did not escalate to full AA release —
recorded as a near-miss in `extra.near_miss_2025_02`, not here, per the trigger-not-met
convention for this section.
