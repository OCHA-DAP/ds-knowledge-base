# Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief Bangladesh — Bangladesh tropical cyclone

## Summary
Bangladesh's National Early Action Protocol (NEAP) for Cyclone, published by the
Anticipation Hub on behalf of the Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief (MoDMR)
in mid-2024, is a government-led coordination protocol rather than a single funded
programme: it ties Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) cyclone danger signals
to a four-phase sequence of multisectoral early actions carried out by government
departments (agriculture, fisheries, livestock) and partners including the Cyclone
Preparedness Programme, Bangladesh Red Crescent Society, IFRC, WFP and FAO. It sits
alongside — not inside — the separately funded OCHA/CERF collective framework, the
IFRC/BDRCS Early Action Protocol, and WFP's own AA cash programme (see Coordination).

## Trigger
Four phases keyed to BMD's numbered cyclone danger signals (1-10):
- **Phase 1 — Early detection** (signals 1-3): technical readiness (radios, torches),
  community awareness via volunteers/radio/TV, stockpiling food/water/medicine,
  emergency helpline activation.
- **Phase 2 — Cyclone formation confirmed** (signal 4, ~120-96 hours before forecast
  landfall): single warning flag raised; public alerts via megaphone/siren/SMS; crop
  harvest-and-store advisories to farmers.
- **Phase 3 — Cyclone intensifies** (signals 5-7, ~72-48 hours before landfall): two
  warning flags; evacuation support for vulnerable people and livestock; shelter WASH
  provisioning (hygiene kits, water purifiers); fishery-pond protection.
- **Phase 4 — Imminent landfall** (signals 8-10, ~36-24 hours before landfall): three
  warning flags; full community evacuation with livestock; child protection and
  psychosocial support; medical aid, food, and cash assistance.

No single lead-time/threshold pair maps to a pre-arranged funding release the way the
OCHA/CERF or IFRC frameworks do — this protocol is a coordination and sequencing
document for actions each partner funds through its own channel.

## Funding & scope
No pre-arranged funding total or forward target-people figure is stated for the
National EAP itself (see `extra.schema_strain`); it coordinates actions funded
separately by government line ministries and by partner AA programmes (BDRCS/IFRC
DREF, WFP's Anticipatory Action Trust Fund, CERF). For national-level context: MoDMR
and WFP's 2025 State of Anticipatory Action report records USD 10.4M mobilised
across all hazards in 2024 (15 activations, 20 districts, ~430,000 people reached)
against an identified 2025 cyclone AA funding need of USD 10M (part of a USD 42M
cross-hazard shortfall) — see `extra.funding_context`.

## Coordination
This is MoDMR's own national coordination protocol, layered above — not a component
of — the other Bangladesh cyclone AA mechanisms tracked in this KB: the OCHA/CERF
collective framework (`frameworks/bgd-cyclone`), the IFRC/BDRCS Early Action Protocol
(`external-frameworks/ifrc/bgd-cyclone.md`), and WFP's own cash AA programme
(`external-frameworks/wfp/bgd-tropical-cyclone.md`). See `extra.coordination` for the
full cross-reference.

## Activations
None known as a real cyclone activation of this specific document. Partners tested it
in a **2 December 2024 simulation exercise** at a cyclone shelter in Barguna district
(~200 community participants) — a readiness drill, not a real activation (see
`extra.simulation_exercise`). Note this is distinct from the real cyclone activations
recorded on the IFRC/BDRCS (Amphan 2020) and WFP (Mocha 2023, Remal 2024) pages, none
of which name this National EAP as their trigger mechanism.
