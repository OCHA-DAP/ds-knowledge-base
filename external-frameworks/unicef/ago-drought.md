# UNICEF — Angola drought

## Summary
UNICEF's country-level anticipatory action activity for drought in Angola, recorded by
the Anticipation Hub as a 2024 UNICEF-coordinated framework/activation tied to the
2023/24 El Niño-induced drought in the southern provinces (Cunene, Huíla, Namibe). The
Hub records US$370,000 invested against a target of 1,200,000 people. No public UNICEF
document specifying the trigger mechanism, funding source breakdown, or precise
activation date was located; this page records only what is independently verifiable.

## Trigger
Not documented in any public source found. The Hub inventory record for this framework
(area-detail-84, project id 346) carries no linked document and no trigger detail. The
broader 2023/24 southern Africa El Niño drought — which this activity responds to — was
characterized regionally by a record mid-season dry spell (50+ days) and rainfall at or
below 40–60% of average across Angola's southwestern provinces, but no source ties a
specific indicator/threshold/lead time to UNICEF's AA trigger design in Angola.

## Funding & scope
US$370,000 invested against a target of 1,200,000 people, per the Anticipation Hub's
2024 record for Angola (identical figures appear in both the Hub's "active frameworks"
and "activations" map layers, suggesting this is a single 2024 activation rather than a
separately-scoped standing framework). The funding source is not stated in the Hub
record. Regionally, UNICEF's ESA Regional Office reported providing US$1.3 million in
Global Humanitarian Thematic Funding (GHTF) across three El Niño-affected countries to
"implement and showcase the value add of anticipatory action for drought" — plausibly
including Angola's share, but this is not confirmed by any source actually opened (the
candidate document returned HTTP 403).

This is separate from OCHA/CERF's non-anticipatory Rapid Response allocation for the
same drought (24-RR-AGO-64769: US$2,997,820 to UNICEF and WFP, approved 2024-04-29,
targeting ~133,000 people in Huíla and Cunene for food security, nutrition and WASH) —
that is emergency response funding, not a pre-arranged AA trigger mechanism.

## Activations
- **2024** — Hub-recorded UNICEF drought activation for Angola: US$370,000 invested,
  1,200,000 people targeted. No more precise date, released-activities detail, or
  outcome report was found in public sources.

No other activations, near-misses, or trigger-not-met events were found in public
reporting.
