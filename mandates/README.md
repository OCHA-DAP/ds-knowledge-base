# mandates/

The institutional footing of the work in this KB: **what OCHA and CERF were set up to do, by whom, and in which document** — read from the primary texts (General Assembly resolutions, Secretary-General's reports and bulletins), not from web summaries or memory. One page per mandate holder; every claim on a page points to a numbered paragraph in an extract under [`raw/mandates/`](../raw/mandates/), so the wording can always be checked.

- [ocha.md](ocha.md) — **OCHA** and the **Emergency Relief Coordinator**: GA resolution 46/182 (1991) — the guiding principles, early warning, the ERC's nine responsibilities, the IASC, consolidated appeals; the 1997 reform (A/51/950) that turned DHA into OCHA and fixed the ERC's three core functions; the 1999 organisation bulletin (ST/SGB/1999/8); the fourth principle (58/114, 2003); the annual resolutions that renew the mandate — and where anticipatory action entered them.
- [resolutions.md](resolutions.md) — **the three annual series**, registered year by year from 1991: the General Assembly omnibus "Strengthening of the coordination of emergency humanitarian assistance of the United Nations" (46/182–79/140; the 2025 draft was withdrawn), the ECOSOC resolution of the same title from the humanitarian affairs segment (1995/56, 1996/33, 2002/32–2024/8; none in 2020, 2025, 2026), and the General Assembly's natural disasters resolution "International cooperation on humanitarian assistance in the field of natural disasters, from relief to development" (54/233–80/113). Where each of the team's themes first entered each series, and the OCHA Reference Guide editions that index them.
- [directly-addressed.md](directly-addressed.md) — **resolutions outside the series whose text tasks or names the ERC/OCHA**: the Security Council's sanctions-related ERC reporting (Somalia 2111→2713, Afghanistan 2615, the 2664 carve-out), the Ukraine emergency-session request (ES-11/1), the IDP resolution; against the "Legislative mandates" list OCHA files with its budget.
- [budget.md](budget.md) — **the programme budget**: the Section 27 / Programme 23 fascicle in which OCHA states its mandate and plans its work each year, the CPC's review, and the two December Fifth Committee resolutions; regular budget versus extrabudgetary figures, 2020–2026, biennial fascicles back to 1998.
- [cerf.md](cerf.md) — **CERF**: the Central Emergency *Revolving* Fund of 46/182 (a $50M loan facility) → the Central Emergency *Response* Fund of 60/124 (2005, grant element, three objectives; ECOSOC 2005/4 recommended the step) → the $1 billion target (71/127, 2016; 79/140, 2024); the operating rules in the current Secretary-General's bulletin (ST/SGB/2020/5): eligibility, the loan and grant elements, the Rapid Response / Underfunded Emergencies split, timelines, reporting, the Advisory Group — and what the GA/bulletin layer does *not* say about anticipatory action.
- [registry.yml](registry.yml) — the machine-readable index behind the tables on these pages (symbol, adoption date from the plenary line, meeting, draft, extract); `scripts/mandates_registry.py` renders it and checks that every extract exists.

## How to read these pages

**Authority levels, highest first.** (1) A **resolution of a principal organ** — General Assembly (`A/RES/…`), Economic and Social Council (`E/RES/…`) or Security Council (`S/RES/…`) — is the Member States' decision: the mandate itself. The Secretariat reads the three as equal in what they bind it to do ("all legislative decisions taken in each of the three intergovernmental bodies are equally binding on the UN Secretariat and recommendatory on Member States, with the notable exception of decisions of the Security Council related to the maintenance of international peace and security that are obligatory for Member States" — OCHA Reference Guide 2009, Preface). (2) A **Secretary-General's bulletin** (`ST/SGB/…`) is the Secretariat's implementing regulation under those resolutions — it says *how* the mandate is run, and a later bulletin abolishes the earlier one. (3) **Secretary-General's reports and budget fascicles** (`A/…`, `A/…/6 (Sect. nn)`) and the **reports of intergovernmental committees** (the CPC, `A/…/16`) propose, plan or account for; they are authoritative on facts and on what the Secretariat intends, but decide nothing until an organ acts on them — the budget fascicle becomes binding only through the Assembly's programme-planning and budget resolutions. (4) Everything else — OCHA/CERF web pages, OCHA publications such as the Reference Guide, guidance notes, the life-saving criteria, our own framework documents — is secretariat practice *under* the above, and a **press release or verbatim record** is evidence of what an organ did, not a decision. Pages here label which level each statement comes from and cite levels 1–3 for the mandate; practice belongs on the framework, pipeline and dataset pages that implement it, and a press release is cited only where the official record is not yet published (the December 2025 withdrawal).

**Paragraph citations.** `46/182 ¶35(e)` means annex paragraph 35, sub-paragraph (e), of `raw/mandates/A_RES_46_182.txt`; `SGB 2020/5 §4.2` means section 4.2 of `raw/mandates/ST_SGB_2020_5.txt`; `79/140 OP59` / `63/139 PP5` are operative and preambular paragraphs in the Reference Guide's convention (used on the registry pages); `2664 OP5` is a Security Council resolution; `A/80/6 (Sect. 27) ¶27.I.10` is a numbered paragraph of a budget fascicle. The annual resolutions renumber their operative paragraphs each year, so a citation always names the year's symbol.

**Scope.** OCHA/the ERC and CERF as mandate holders; the three annual resolution series; the other resolutions that task the ERC/OCHA; the programme budget. The bodies 46/182 created alongside them — the ERC, the IASC, the consolidated appeal — are covered inline on the OCHA page rather than given pages of their own. ## Sources and refresh

Extracts under `raw/mandates/` are `pdftotext -layout` of the official English PDFs fetched from the UN Official Document System's symbol endpoint (`https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=<url-encoded symbol>&l=E&t=pdf` — works for `A/RES/…`, `E/RES/…` from 2012, `S/RES/2664 (2022)` with the year, `A/80/6 (Sect. 27)`, `A/80/16`), from CERF's document pages, or, for ECOSOC resolutions before 2012, from the Council's website PDFs and official-records volumes (`E/<year>/99`). Exceptions, each stated in a bracketed header line inside the file: **46/182** (ODS holds only an image scan; text from the former un.org HTML copy, OCR-checked), **47/168** and **ECOSOC 1995/56** (image scans, tesseract OCR), **ECOSOC 2004/50** (cut from E/2004/99 because the Council's website link serves the wrong resolution), the **CPC reports** (only the Programme 23 chapter is kept) and the **biennial budget fascicles 1998–2019** (overview only). Every document the pages cite is in [registry.yml](registry.yml); `scripts/mandates_registry.py --check` fails if an extract is missing, and without `--check` it re-renders the tables. The corpus grows by three resolutions, one fascicle and one CPC chapter a year, plus a bulletin a decade; the refresh recipe is at the end of each page. The press release of 10 December 2025 (GA/12742) is kept because the verbatim record of that meeting was not yet in ODS; replace it when A/80/PV.60 appears.
