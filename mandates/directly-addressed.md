# Resolutions addressed to the ERC or OCHA outside the annual series

**In one paragraph.** Beyond the three annual series on [resolutions.md](resolutions.md), the ERC and OCHA are tasked or named in a scatter of other decisions: Security Council resolutions that require the ERC to report on humanitarian access under sanctions regimes (Somalia since 2013, Afghanistan since 2021, every regime since 2664 of 2022), one General Assembly emergency-session resolution that asked the ERC for a report on Ukraine within 30 days (ES-11/1 OP13), and the Assembly's annual IDP resolution, which affirms the ERC's "central role" in coordinating protection of and assistance to internally displaced persons (80/211 OP16). This page lists them two ways and keeps the two apart: **(a) the textual test** — resolutions whose own words address or name the ERC/OCHA, found by searching the UN's machine-readable resolution sets (Security Council 2000–2026, General Assembly 74th–80th sessions, ECOSOC 2021–2025) and read in place — and **(b) OCHA's own list** — the "Legislative mandates" paragraph that the Office files with each year's budget fascicle, which is broader (it includes thematic Council resolutions on the protection of civilians, conflict and hunger, and the safety of humanitarian personnel that never name the ERC) and changes every year. Neither list is the mandate; 46/182 and the annual resolutions are. These are the places where other organs have given the Office specific jobs.

## How this list was made, and what it does not cover

**The textual test.** Every English resolution in the UNxml corpus (<https://github.com/UNxml>: `SCresolutions`, `GAresolutions`, `ECOSOCresolutions`) was searched for "Emergency Relief Coordinator", "Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs" and "Under-Secretary-General for Humanitarian Affairs", the hits were read, and each was classed as one of three things: the text **tasks** the ERC/OCHA (requests, decides); the text **affirms a role** of the ERC/OCHA (emphasizes, welcomes, takes note of); or the mention is **incidental** (OCHA inside a list of agencies asked to cooperate with a regional organisation, or a cooperation-with-UNHCR clause). The first two classes are tabled below with their paragraph; the third is listed by symbol only. Coverage follows the corpus: Security Council resolutions from 2000, General Assembly resolutions from the 74th session (2019–2020), ECOSOC resolutions from 2021. Nothing earlier was searched this way — for the period 1991–2015 the OCHA Reference Guide's chapter on the ERC (2009 ed. ch. 9; 2015 ed. ch. 10) is the index, with the caveat in its own preface that it "does not include intergovernmental decisions adopted for specific humanitarian emergencies or disasters" — which is precisely what most of the Security Council items below are. Presidential statements (S/PRST) also name the ERC and OCHA — 18 of them between 2000 and 2018 in the corpus, from the Council's appreciation of OCHA's work on IDPs in Georgia (S/PRST/2000/16) to the recurring aide-memoire on the protection of civilians "the result of consultation between the Security Council and the Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs" (S/PRST/2009/1, 2010/25, 2014/3, 2015/23, 2018/18) — but they are statements, not resolutions, and are not counted here.

**What the user's criterion includes that the Guide excludes.** The rule for this page is textual: if the operative wording is directed to the ERC/USG or to OCHA as an organisation, it belongs here whether or not it is country-specific. That is why the Somalia sanctions chain, which the Reference Guide would leave out, is the longest entry.

**Three strands.** (1) **Somalia, 2013–2023.** Starting with 2111 (2013) OP23, every renewal of the Somalia (later Al-Shabaab) sanctions regime asks the ERC to report to the Council "on the delivery of humanitarian assistance in Somalia and on any impediments to the delivery of humanitarian assistance" — the reporting duty attached to the humanitarian exemption first created by resolution 1916 (2010), which addressed the UN Humanitarian Coordinator for Somalia rather than the ERC. The ERC's report is due each October (2182 OP42 "by 1 October 2015" … 2713 OP30 "by 15 October 2024"). (2) **Afghanistan, 2021.** 2615 (2021) OP2 asks the ERC to brief the Council every six months on humanitarian delivery under the exception to the 1988 regime, "including on any available information regarding payments of funds to, or for the benefit of, designated individuals or entities, any diversion of funds by the same, risk management and due diligence processes in place, and any obstacles", and asks providers to feed the ERC within 60 days. (3) **All regimes, 2022.** 2664 (2022) OP5 generalises the model: the ERC is to "brief or arrange a briefing for each relevant Committee within its mandate 11 months from the date of adoption of this resolution and every 12 months afterwards on the delivery of humanitarian assistance and other activities that support basic human needs" under the carve-out, and 2761 (2024) extends the carve-out for the ISIL (Da'esh)/Al-Qaida regime while recalling those briefings. Outside sanctions, 2401 (2018) OP9 "takes note with appreciation of the five requests identified by the Emergency Relief Coordinator on 11 January 2018 during his mission to Syria" — the Council endorsing a list the ERC drew up, rather than tasking him.

**Why it matters for data work.** The 2615 and 2664 briefings are the standing occasions on which the ERC reports on humanitarian delivery, diversion risk and access under sanctions; the Somalia reports are an annual series since 2014. They are the Council-side counterpart of the ECOSOC and Assembly reports and draw on the same OCHA information pipelines (FTS, access monitoring, pooled-fund data). The "wrong" resolutions to cite for OCHA's sanctions role are the thematic ones (2417 on conflict and hunger, 2573 on objects indispensable to civilian survival): they are in OCHA's own list (§3) but do not address the ERC.

## 3. OCHA's own list — the "Legislative mandates" paragraph of the budget fascicle

Since the 2021 budget (A/75/6 (Sect. 27)), each year's fascicle has carried a paragraph that "provides all mandates entrusted to the programme" (A/80/6 (Sect. 27) ¶27.I.10). It is the Secretariat's statement of what it considers its legislative basis, reviewed each year; the 2020 fascicle (A/74/6) had a prose "Mandates and background" paragraph instead (¶27.2). The 2026 list is:

- **General Assembly:** 46/182 and 79/140 (the series); 47/120 A and B (Agenda for Peace); 52/12 (the 1997 reform); 52/167 (safety and security of humanitarian personnel); 60/1 (2005 World Summit Outcome); 62/208 and 79/226 (comprehensive policy reviews); 63/147 (new international humanitarian order); 68/1, 72/305, 75/290 A and B, 78/285 (strengthening of ECOSOC); 69/313 (Addis Ababa Action Agenda); 70/1 (2030 Agenda); 74/306 (COVID-19); 76/264 (state of global food insecurity); 77/288 (Chernobyl); 78/185 (refugees in Africa); 78/205 (IDPs); 79/1 (Pact for the Future); 79/138 (safety of humanitarian personnel); 79/139 (natural disasters).
- **ECOSOC:** 2024/8.
- **Security Council:** 1265 (1999), 1894 (2009), 2222 (2015), 2286 (2016), 2417 (2018), 2474 (2019), 2475 (2019), 2532 (2020), 2573 (2021), 2615 (2021), 2616 (2021), 2664 (2022), 2690 (2023), 2727 (2024), 2729 (2024), 2730 (2024), 2732 (2024), 2742 (2024), 2753 (2024), 2755 (2024), 2759 (2024), 2760 (2024), 2761 (2024), 2765 (2024).

**Reading it against the textual test.** Of the Council resolutions OCHA lists, only 2615, 2664 and 2761 address the ERC; the rest are the thematic protection-of-civilians, safety-of-personnel, conflict-and-hunger and missing-persons texts (1265, 1894, 2222, 2286, 2417, 2474, 2475, 2532, 2573, 2616, 2690, 2730) and the year's mission and sanctions renewals (the 2024 run from 2727 to 2765). Conversely, OCHA's list omits the Somalia reporting chain that *does* task the ERC. The two lists answer different questions — "what does the Office treat as its basis" versus "where has an organ told the ERC to do something" — and both are kept. The lists for 2021–2025 are in the earlier fascicles' extracts ([budget.md](budget.md) has the table); the Council entries turn over almost completely each year, the Assembly core (46/182, the series, 47/120, 52/12, 52/167, 60/1, 70/1) does not.
