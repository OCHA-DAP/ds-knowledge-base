# Burkina Faso drought — 2025-02-28

> The authoritative trigger is the OCHA/CERF framework document (`framework_doc`, "BFA AAF REVISION 2", 28 Feb 2025). The doc itself publishes its analysis code at `ocha-dap/pa-aa-bfa-drought` (`code_ref`); this page explains the design, it does not redefine it. Superseded by the [2026-04-17](2026-04-17.md) revision.

## Summary
Seasonal drought AA over four ADM1 regions of Burkina Faso (Boucle du Mouhoun, Centre-Nord, Nord, Sahel), the **second revision** of the collective framework. Three **independent** triggers, each releasing its own share of a pre-arranged **CERF envelope of up to US$15M**: two SEAS5 forecast triggers (March, looking at Jun–Aug rainfall; July, looking at Aug–Oct rainfall) and one JRC ASAP end-of-season biomass observational trigger (October). Partial activation of the action plan is explicitly allowed — a single window releases $7.2–7.8M, and only a two-window season spends the full $15M. Targets more than 300,000 people through FAO, WFP, UNICEF, UNHCR and UNFPA. Approved by the ERC in March 2025 and valid for two years from approval. Superseded by the [17 April 2026 revision](2026-04-17.md), which cut to 2 regions / 4 ADM2 provinces, raised the SEAS5 area threshold from 10% to 50%, and switched to a single all-in disbursement.

## Method
**Trigger 1 (T1, March):** the ECMWF SEAS5 seasonal forecast issued in March is checked for total JJA (Jun–Aug) precipitation; the trigger fires if at least 10% of the AOI area is forecast in the lowest historical quintile. A pixel counts as "lowest quintile" if its value is below the 20th percentile of that pixel's historical values *for the same valid period and the same publication month, since 1981*. **Trigger 2 (T2, July):** identical mechanism, issued in July, checked for ASO (Aug–Oct) precipitation. **Trigger 3 (T3, October):** the JRC ASAP platform issues a Level-4 alert (end-of-season biomass) for agricultural **and** pastoral areas in at least one of the four AOI regions during October. Each trigger is evaluated independently (logical OR) and releases the sector envelopes attached to its own window — this is a **split, not all-in**, framework. OCHA/CHD monitors all three and notifies the pilot team by email at each monitoring moment.

## Trigger logic
- **Keys off:** SEAS5 seasonal-precipitation quintile (forecast, T1 + T2); JRC ASAP end-of-season biomass alert (observational, T3).
- **Decision rule (plain language):** On 5 March, check the SEAS5 forecast for JJA rainfall — if at least 10% of the four-region AOI area falls in the lowest (driest) quintile of that pixel's record since 1981, T1 fires and money is released for food security, protection and WASH. On 5 July, repeat the same check against the ASO forecast — if met, T2 fires and money is released for food security, protection and nutrition. Through October (checks on 15 Oct, 25 Oct and 5 Nov, each ~5 days after a dekad closes), check the JRC ASAP platform — if at least one of the four regions carries a Level-4 (most severe) end-of-season biomass alert for both cropland and rangeland, T3 fires and money is released for food security, nutrition and protection. The three checks are independent; any one firing releases that window's share of the CERF envelope without needing the others.
- **Activation structure:** Three independent windows, OR logic, **partial (per-window) activation** — `all_in: false`. Retained combinations and their budgets: March only $7.8M; July only $7.2M; October only $7.4M; March + July $15M; March + October up to $15M (the October budget is trimmed by $200k to stay inside the envelope). A July + October combination was deliberately dropped in this revision to avoid duplicate activities. (The subsequent [2026-04-17](2026-04-17.md) revision consolidated to 2 windows with a single all-in disbursement.)
- **Calibration:** Return-period based, tuned to CERF's 3–5 year guideline for the *overall* framework. Each forecast trigger (T1, T2) individually ~10-year return period; both forecast triggers together ~6-year; the observational trigger (T3) ~4-year; overall framework (any window) ~3.1-year. The repo's calibration notebook reaches the same threshold from the other direction: "the threshold that corresponds to the requested forecast RP (around 6 years combined), is 20th, which is the lower quintile" (`analysis/ecmwf_switch_anomaly.md`).
- **Authoritative source:** the February 2025 PDF (`framework_doc`, "BFA AAF REVISION 2"). The repo (`code_ref`) implements/derives it — the doc names the repo in its own footnote 3.
- **Operated by:** OCHA/CHD ("Les trois déclencheurs seront surveillés par le CHD"); no external operator.

## Trigger windows

**Counting rule:** three independent activation windows (two forecast, one observational), each with its own sector envelope. `n_windows = 3`.

| window | basis | indicator | threshold | lead time | return period | releases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 (March) | forecast | SEAS5 JJA seasonal precipitation, historical quintile | ≥10% of AOI area in lowest quintile (pixel < 20th percentile of its record since 1981, same valid period + publication month) | issued March, targets Jun–Aug; checked 5 Mar | ~10-year | $7.8M — food security, protection, WASH |
| T2 (July) | forecast | SEAS5 ASO seasonal precipitation, historical quintile | ≥10% of AOI area in lowest quintile (same definition) | issued July, targets Aug–Oct; checked 5 Jul | ~10-year | $7.2M — food security, protection, nutrition |
| T3 (October) | observational | JRC ASAP end-of-season biomass alert (agricultural **and** pastoral zones) | Level-4 alert in ≥1 of the 4 AOI regions during October | end-of-season; checked 15 Oct, 25 Oct, 5 Nov | ~4-year | $7.4M — food security, nutrition, protection |

Forecast triggers combined (either firing) ~6-year; overall framework (any window) ~3.1-year. Two windows firing in one season spends the full $15M; a single window spends $7.2–7.8M — hence the doc's distinction between the *overall* return period (~3.1 yr) and the *effective* return period for spending the whole envelope.

## Key decisions & rationale
- **IRI → SEAS5, because of the *producer*, not the science.** The forecast triggers were switched to ECMWF SEAS5 "en raison des incertitudes opérationnels de leur production" — i.e. operational uncertainty about continued IRI production, not a skill finding. Everything else about the forecast mechanism (area fraction, monitoring dates, target period) was deliberately carried over unchanged.
- **Absolute rainfall → per-pixel historical percentile, after working-group pushback.** The first SEAS5 proposal used absolute ensemble-mean precipitation. The working group objected that typical rainfall varies too much across the AOI for an absolute comparison to be meaningful, so rainfall was recast as a per-pixel historical percentile — also the closer analogue to the old IRI tercile-probability trigger.
- **ASAP chosen for publication reliability.** The previous observational trigger suffered unexpected data-publication delays; ASAP was investigated and adopted for the reliability of its publication schedule and the relevance of its drought processing.
- **The observational trigger was deliberately made *easier* to fire than the forecast triggers** (~4-year vs ~10-year RP): the working group picked the lower-left option on the trigger-combination chart to get a higher probability of triggering on observations than on forecasts, because observations are expected to be more accurate at detecting drought conditions.
- **Thresholds set from the *overall* return period.** Combinations were selected so that the overall framework RP (~3.1 years) sits inside CERF's 3–5 year guideline, rather than by optimising each window alone.
- **10%-of-AOI area threshold.** A permissive area-coverage bar inherited from the IRI pilot (and matching `constants.perc_area = 10`); tightened to 50% in the 2026 revision.
- **Three independent, partially-activating windows.** Splitting the forecast trigger across March/JJA and July/ASO plus an end-of-season observational backstop lets the framework respond to signals appearing at different points in the season, with each window funded and able to fire on its own. Protection activities were added to *all three* windows in this revision.
- **Accuracy was measured, not designed to.** Because the working group never agreed target years in advance, the doc computes skill only after the fact, against years with a CERF rapid-response allocation or an EM-DAT entry touching the AOI (2011, 2014, 2017, 2019, 2022, assessed from 2006).
