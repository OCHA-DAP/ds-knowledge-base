# Chad Flood — 2025-07-31

> The canonical trigger is the code at `code_ref`; this page explains it, it does not redefine it.

## Summary

The Chad anticipatory action framework for riverine flooding triggers on daily GloFAS v4 ensemble-mean discharge forecasts for the Chari River at N'Djamena. Two windows share one threshold of 4,542 m³/s: a readiness ("mobilisation") trigger within 14 days and an action trigger within 10 days. Either one releases the pre-arranged CERF envelope (max USD 4M) for UNHCR (shelter/NFI), UNICEF (WASH), FAO and WFP (food security), to act in both N'Djamena and Mayo-Kebbi Est (Bongor zone). The framework was first activated in 2024; this 31 July 2025 revision follows that activation and covers the 2025 and 2026 seasons.

## Method

Each day CDH reads the GloFAS ensemble forecast (CDS/EWDS API, `cems-glofas-forecast`, `ensemble_perturbed_forecasts`, `dis24`, lead days 1–14) for the N'Djamena station ("Chari A Ndjamena - Ex Fort-Lamy-", downstream of the Logone confluence; ~12.125°N, 15.025°E in the code) and takes the **ensemble mean** over members (the PDF's indicator, "la moyenne des ensembles"; footnote 7 also loosely labels it the "control forecast"). If any forecast day within the window reaches the threshold, that window's trigger is active. CDH informs OCHA Chad, the CERF secretariat and the AA working group by email and WhatsApp; DRE and ANAM must confirm within 24 hours. On a readiness trigger (or an action trigger with no prior readiness) CERF sends approval letters immediately and interventions can start.

The threshold comes from the GloFAS reanalysis/reforecast analysis (`analysis/glofas_reforecast_2025.md`): the ~4-yr RP discharge at N'Djamena. Geographic risk targeting uses FloodScan SFED (1998–2023) overlaid on WorldPop; N'Djamena has the most exposed people (238,635) followed by Mayo-Kebbi Est (64,874).

## Trigger logic

- **Keys off:** GloFAS v4 ensemble-mean river discharge (m³/s) at the N'Djamena station on the Chari.
- **Decision rule (plain language):** Each day from July, look at every forecast day up to the maximum lead time. If any is ≥ 4,542 m³/s within 14 days, the readiness trigger fires; if any is ≥ 4,542 m³/s within 10 days, the action trigger fires. Either one launches activities in both N'Djamena and Mayo-Kebbi Est (single calibration on the N'Djamena station).
- **Activation structure:** two sequential windows, same threshold, OR logic; an action trigger without a prior readiness trigger also releases funds. The code reports "readiness" and/or "action" (French emails: "MOBILISATION ACTIVÉ" / "ACTION ACTIVÉ" / "NON ACTIVÉ"). Code treats lead days 0–10 / 0–14 inclusive (`lead_days` = valid date − issue date).
- **Calibration:** ~4-yr RP at the threshold; estimated RP of 3.0 yr (≤14 days) and 4.0 yr (≤10 days) per the PDF table; backtest 2003–2022. Overall RP 3.0 yr, overall activation probability 33% (captions).
- **Authoritative source:** the 2025-07-31 framework PDF (`framework_doc`); the repo implements and validates it.
- **Operated by:** OCHA CHD (automated script) with DRE/ANAM confirmation (PDF §4 protocol). The repo is the production monitor — the Databricks job on `main`, not the keep-awake branch.

## Trigger windows

| window | basis | indicator | threshold | lead time | return period | releases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Readiness (Mobilisation) | forecast | GloFAS ensemble-mean discharge, Chari R. at N'Djamena | ≥ 4,542 m³/s | ≤ 14 days | ~3.0 yr | CERF approval letters sent; agencies can start immediately |
| Action | forecast | GloFAS ensemble-mean discharge, Chari R. at N'Djamena | ≥ 4,542 m³/s | ≤ 10 days | ~4.0 yr | Same envelope; fires alone or after readiness |

Backtest (2003–2022 GloFAS reforecast, `analysis/glofas_reforecast_2025.md`): the action window (≤10 d) would have fired in 2003, 2005, 2010, 2012 and 2022 (5 years ≈ RP 4 yr, 25%); the readiness window (≤14 d) in those five plus 2007 and 2018 (7 years ≈ RP 3 yr, 37%). The PDF's statistics tables are images and the vision captions garble the per-window year lists (see discrepancies); the RP and probability columns agree with the analysis.

## Key decisions & rationale

- **Single station for both provinces:** a Bongor (Logone) station was tested and did not improve performance; the N'Djamena station would have triggered for all five most severe Mayo-Kebbi Est flood years (2024, 2012, 2022, 2010, 2003). Trade-off: Mayo-Kebbi Est is upstream and can flood before the signal.
- **Same threshold for both windows:** the 14-day window yields an effective RP of ~3.0 yr, meeting the CERF ≥3-yr readiness requirement without a second threshold.
- **Ensemble mean:** simple and reproducible against the reforecast analysis (the GloFAS web interface no longer shows numeric values, so the CDS/EWDS API is the check).
- **Early-bias accepted:** GloFAS peaks ~31 days before observed peaks, so even a late trigger leaves roughly a month.
- **Funding:** one CERF envelope (USD 4M: 25% shelter/NFI, 25% WASH, 50% food security); funds are automatically disbursed once a trigger is met.
