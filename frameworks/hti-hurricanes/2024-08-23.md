# Haiti hurricanes — 2024-08-23 (Pilote, Version #1)

## Summary
National tropical-cyclone AA pilot keyed on **both** wind speed AND 2-day national-mean rainfall while a storm is within 230 km of Haiti. Three stages (Mobilisation / Anticipatory Action / Early Response) with distinct thresholds and lead times. CERF up to $4M + co-financing $2.5M. Validated RC/HC 18 Aug 2024, ERC 23 Aug 2024. CERF eligibility runs for **two years from ERC approval — until 2026-08-23** (imminent as of this writing).

## Method
NHC forecasts/observed tracks give wind + proximity; CHIRPS-GEFS (forecast) and IMERG (observed) give 2-day national-mean rainfall. Wind AND rain must both cross within the stage's lead-time window, with the storm center within 230 km of Haiti. Monitoring + certification by OCHA/CHD (pipeline folded into this repo; historically GitHub-Actions-driven, now a Databricks job — see Monitoring).

## Trigger logic
- **Keys off:** max sustained wind (kt) AND 2-day total precipitation (national mean), gated by distance ≤230 km.
- **Decision rule:** each stage fires when wind ≥ its threshold AND rain ≥ its threshold within its lead window, while the storm center is ≤230 km from Haiti.
- **Activation structure:** three time-ordered stages (Mobilisation → Anticipatory Action → Early Response); since Mobilisation and Action share the same threshold, an Action firing implies Mobilisation also fired (simultaneously or with a delay).
- **Calibration:** the annex ("Analyses historiques du déclencheur", PDF pp.20-22) describes the full method — EM-DAT (impact) + IBTrACS (track/wind) + CHIRPS (rain) for every Atlantic system since 2000, brute-force swept over a grid of distance (D: 0-500km), wind (S: 10-180kt), rain-aggregation days (N: 1-3 + full transit), area-coverage (X: 10-90%) and rain threshold (P: 10-170mm), scoring each candidate trigger by "total affected population captured" and keeping only combinations with a global return period ≥3 years. AND-logic (wind AND rain) was found "beaucoup plus efficace" than OR-logic or wind-only triggers (footnote 24). A parallel sweep replayed historical NOAA/CHIRPS-GEFS *forecasts* (not just observations) to set the forecast-stage thresholds, with a 48h "heure limite" cutoff before closest approach informing why forecast thresholds are set stricter than the preliminary observational-only trigger. IMERG replaced CHIRPS for the observational stage because of CHIRPS's >5-day publication lag. The doc notes explicitly that **no combined wind+rain+distance threshold at RP≥3yr could have captured Jeanne 2004** (~170,184 affected) despite its impact — an acknowledged limitation of the design.
- **Authoritative source:** 2024 PDF (Tableau 4); the repo's historical calibration notebooks (`exploration/threestage_trigger.md`, `exploration/hist_fcast_triggers.md`) implement the same method, though the exact published backtest table (annex, p.20) was not re-run/re-verified against current notebook output for this ingestion.
- **Operated by:** OCHA/CHD (not external). **As of source_sha 1187895, the live monitoring pipeline in this repo no longer implements this v1 trigger at all** — see Sources & repo completeness and Monitoring below.

## Trigger windows

| window (PDF → code) | basis | wind | 2-day rain | lead time | return period |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mobilisation → readiness | forecast | ≥ 64 kt | ≥ 42 mm | ≤ 120 h | 3.4 yr |
| Anticipatory Action → action | forecast | ≥ 64 kt | ≥ 42 mm | ≤ 72 h | 4.8 yr |
| Early Response → obsv | observational | ≥ 50 kt | ≥ 70 mm | ~+1 day | 4.0 yr |

(All gated by ≤230 km. Obs rain (70mm) > forecast rain (42mm) corrects CHIRPS-GEFS↔IMERG bias. Global combined return period: 3.0 yr, the CERF-eligibility target.)

**Simulated/backtested record** (PDF annex p.20, EM-DAT since 2000 — distinct from the real 2025 activation below): would-have-triggered systems include Matthew 2016 (mobilisation+action+obsv all True, 316,839 affected), Isaac 2012 (all True), Tomas 2010 (all True), and Ivan 2004 (mobilisation+action True, obsv False). High-impact systems the trigger would **not** have caught: Jeanne 2004 (~170,184 affected — see calibration note above), Sandy 2012, Ike 2008 (~129,508), Noël 2007 (~90,000), Gustav 2008 (~50,000), Hanna 2008 (~50,000), Laura 2020 (~38,000), Grace 2021 (~46,870).

## Historical activations
**Activated 21 October 2025** for **Hurricane Melissa** (Category 5) — the first activation of the v1 framework. Both forecast windows fired: the **readiness** trigger on the 21 Oct 15:00 UTC NHC advisory (~4.9 days to forecast closest approach), then the **action** trigger on the 22 Oct 09:00 UTC advisory (~2.9 days), per the monitoring record (`ds-aa-hti-hurricanes/monitoring/hti_fcast_monitoring.parquet`, produced by the v1 pipeline that was live at the time and has since been removed from `main`). Within one hour of the 21 Oct (readiness) activation the pre-agreed USD 4M CERF envelope was released to WFP, UNICEF and IOM to support >100,000 people in Haiti's high-risk southern areas ([OCHA, 19 Nov 2025](https://www.unocha.org/news/forecast-frontline-how-acting-early-helped-people-prepare-hurricane-melissa); same date/amount/reach in [CERF, *CERF at the forefront of anticipatory action*](https://cerf.un.org/sites/default/files/resources/CERF_Atforefront_AA.pdf)). The OCHA article describes the trigger as the "next three days or so" forecast, conflating the readiness and action windows. With WFP's support the Government sent out **3.5 million early-warning messages**, and WFP made anticipatory cash transfers to at-risk families ([WFP](https://www.wfp.org/stories/hope-haiti-lessons-frontlines-hunger-crisis) — no dollar amount or beneficiary count is published for the Haiti cash component). DGPC subsequently reported 46 deaths and ~250,000 people affected. This activation directly motivated the melissa-exposure trigger redesign now on `main` (see the in-development [2026-06-09](2026-06-09.md) successor page) — note that redesign work has since progressed further than that page currently records (canonical thresholds are now committed to `main`'s `constants.py` and are live in production monitoring, see discrepancies).

## Key decisions & rationale
Wind AND rain (not wind alone or an OR) to catch wet/slow storms — the annex explicitly found AND-logic far more effective at capturing high-impact systems without excess false positives. Added an observational post-landfall trigger for rapid intensification / late track shifts missed by forecasts. Higher obs rain threshold (70mm vs 42mm) corrects forecast↔observation (CHIRPS-GEFS↔IMERG) bias. ~3-yr global RP target for CERF eligibility (CERF only funds "extraordinary, not annual" crises). Forecast thresholds set stricter than a naive observational-derived preliminary trigger specifically to control false positives from the less-certain long-lead portions of the forecast (the 48h "heure limite" cutoff). The design knowingly accepts it cannot catch every high-impact storm (Jeanne 2004 being the clearest miss) as the cost of holding a ~3-year return period.
