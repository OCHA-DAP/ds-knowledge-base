## Summary

This is the endorsed 2026 revision of Nigeria's flood anticipatory action framework, focused on **Adamawa state** along the Benue river. The action trigger is a **multi-gauge consensus design**: fires when ≥6 of 10 selected Google GRRR gauges simultaneously exceed their individual 4-year return-period threshold, replacing the 2025 single-station OR logic (GloFAS OR GRRR at one Wuroboki gauge). A **GloFAS readiness trigger** (ensemble reforecast mean > 3,132 m³/s at Wuroboki, lead time ≤13 days) gives partners advance notice ahead of the action trigger, **OR**-ed with a GloFAS reanalysis branch — mandated by the published document and implemented in code. The **NHF flash-flood** component (observational, BAY states) is retargeted for 2026 to 4 LGAs (Mobbar, Maiduguri, Jere, Geidam — NiHSA composite score) with new per-LGA FloodScan-exposure thresholds from the framework document, validated against the operational data (equal per-LGA probability, combined ~3.5-yr RP). Email dispatch now runs through **Listmonk/ocha-relay** rather than raw SMTP. The document pre-arranges **US$6M** — CERF $5M (WFP, IOM, UNICEF, WHO, FAO) + NHF $1M (national NGOs LESGO, NRCS, HARAF) — plus **$2M WFP co-financing** for MPCA, to protect **up to 764,864 people** in the 7 LGAs. CERF funding is pre-arranged for one activation over two seasons (2026 and 2027; arrangements in place until mid-2028); NHF funds are expected to be used by end-2026, reprogrammed to flash floods (Scenario 2) or other priorities (Scenario 3) if the riverine trigger does not fire. As of **2026-08-10/11 the full 2026 design is merged to `main` and live**: `monitoring.yml` (riverine) runs daily at 20:00 UTC and `flash-monitoring.yml` runs daily at 01:30 UTC (timed to the floodexposure chain), both with `STAGE=prod`; each stream's production audience is deliberately limited to two team members (soak test) pending migration of the full distribution list.

## Method

The repo implements the trigger directly in `src/constants.py` and evaluates it via `pipelines/check_forecasts.py` → `src/monitoring/etl.evaluate_trigger` → `pipelines/send_emails.py` (intended daily; see Monitoring for why it isn't). The structured workflow notebooks that derived the design (gauge selection, RP calibration, readiness lead-time evaluation — `exploration/2026/cerf/workflow/01`–`07`) are **not present on this branch**; they sit on the separate, also-unmerged `feat/niger-benue-multistate-monitoring` (confirmed present there — it is the branch `deploy-app-cron.yml` checks out). This page therefore describes the trigger as implemented in `src/constants.py`/`etl.py`, with the design rationale cross-checked against that branch's session note.

The selected Adamawa action trigger: **≥6 of 10 GRRR gauges simultaneously exceed their individual 4-yr empirical Weibull RP threshold on at least one wet-season day** (per-gauge thresholds ~1,101–1,117 m³/s for 9 main-channel gauges, 142.7 m³/s for the outlier). The readiness trigger combines a GloFAS reforecast branch (Option A) with a reanalysis OR-branch, both evaluated against 3,132 m³/s. The observational NHF flash-flood window is retargeted to 4 BAY-state LGAs with framework-document exposure thresholds (see `extra.nhf_flash_flood`).

## Trigger logic

- **Keys off:** Google GRRR reforecast streamflow at 10 gauges in Adamawa (action); GloFAS ensemble reforecast mean AND latest GloFAS reanalysis at Wuroboki (readiness); FloodScan SFED x WorldPop per-LGA population exposure (NHF flash flood).
- **Decision rule (plain language):** The **action** trigger fires if at least 6 of the 10 selected GRRR gauges simultaneously report streamflow above their individual 4-year return-period threshold on the same forecast valid day. The **readiness** trigger fires when either the GloFAS ensemble-mean forecast at Wuroboki exceeds 3,132 m³/s at a lead time of ≤13 days, **or** the latest available GloFAS reanalysis (fetched by walking back from −2 to −7 days to find an available date) exceeds the same threshold — an earlier, lower-bar pre-positioning signal that does not guarantee an action activation will follow. The reanalysis branch is implemented in code (commit 5d44ff8) as a near-real-time safety net for reforecast misses, "per the framework doc" in the commit's own words. The **NHF flash-flood** window fires when the 3-day rolling mean of FloodScan-derived population exposure in any of the 4 retargeted LGAs (Mobbar, Maiduguri, Jere, Geidam) exceeds its per-LGA threshold; an advisory email goes out at 80% of any threshold.
- **Activation structure:** Two riverine windows for Adamawa — readiness (GloFAS, intended to fire first) and action (GRRR multi-gauge) — plus the parallel NHF flash-flood window. Emails distinguish three states (`action` > `readiness` > "not activated"), sent via Listmonk whenever a trigger fires or it's a Monday. Only the action state routes to the `nga:trigger` list; readiness goes to `nga:info` alongside the routine update.
- **Calibration:** Empirical Weibull RP from wet-season (Aug–Nov) annual maxima, 1998–2023 (GRRR) / 2003–2022 (GloFAS reforecast). Action trigger **4.5-yr** RP (k=6 fire years in n=26); readiness option A **3.0-yr** RP (k=7 in n=20). Verified against the design note on the sibling branch (`exploration/2026/cerf/notes/2026-06-18.md`), which is not reachable from this checkout — see discrepancies. Readiness POD vs the four action years with reforecast coverage is 0.75 (misses 2018); lead time over the action trigger is highly uneven (+46 d in 2019, +2 to +13 d in 2022/2012).
- **Authoritative source:** `trigger_source: framework_doc` — the published document (28 Aug 2026). Code (`src/constants.py`, `src/monitoring/etl.py`, pipelines) agrees on thresholds, gauges, LGAs and the readiness OR-branch; the readiness lead-time wording (doc 'first 12 days' vs code ≤13) and the flash-flood combined RP (doc ~4 yr vs ~3.5 yr validated) differ — see discrepancies.
- **Operated by:** null — analysis and monitoring code by OCHA CHD, intended to run via GitHub Actions in this repo, not a third-party system. As of `source_sha` neither the branch workflow nor the pre-merge shim is actually scheduled (see Monitoring).

## Monitoring

The daily pipeline (`pipelines/check_forecasts.py` → `save_plots.py` → `send_emails.py`, GHA `monitoring.yml`, cron `0 20 * * *`) implements the endorsed 2026 design — live from `main` since 2026-08-10. **Action check:** all 10 endorsed GRRR gauges fetched in one Google API call, per-gauge 4-yr RP thresholds hardcoded in `src/constants.py` (`ACTION_GAUGE_THRESHOLDS`), fires on ≥6 simultaneous same-valid-day exceedances (`etl.evaluate_trigger`). **Readiness check:** GloFAS forecast at Wuroboki to a 13-day lead (CDS `cems-glofas-forecast`) OR the latest available `cems-glofas-historical` reanalysis (walking back up to 7 days; a missing reanalysis logs a warning but never breaks the run), both vs 3,132 m³/s — the reanalysis fetch was recently adapted (commit ff36c17) to a restructured CDS schema from the Aug-2026 GloFAS v5 rollout, while staying pinned to `system_version: version_4_0` for calibration consistency. Emails go via **Listmonk/ocha-relay** (default `base_campaign` template; lists resolved by tag — `nga:info`, `nga:trigger`, `nga:test`; see `infrastructure/comms-listmonk.md`); `STAGE != prod` routes all sends to the test list with the template's test banner.

**Deployment status — LIVE (2026-08-10).** The path in: shim PR #38 merged 2026-08-10 morning and validated the full GHA chain in soak mode (one linux-only fix surfaced: eccodes/cfgrib segfault at interpreter teardown after successful completion — worked around with `os._exit(0)` in `check_forecasts.py`); PR #37 then merged to `main`, `monitoring.yml` was re-enabled (it had been `disabled_inactivity` since 2025-12-08), and the shim was deleted so exactly one cron runs: `monitoring.yml`, daily 20:00 UTC from `main`. `STAGE` repo var = `prod` → real Listmonk campaigns to the prod lists (`nga:info` 113 / `nga:trigger` 114), which deliberately contain only two team members as a soak-test audience; the test list (`nga:test` 115) remains for `STAGE=dev` runs. Charts are restyled with HDX design tokens. DB writes remain dev-stage.

**Flash-flood monitoring — LIVE (2026-08-11, repo PR #39).** A second daily pipeline (`pipelines/monitor_flash_flood.py`, GHA `flash-monitoring.yml`, cron `30 1 * * *` — timed to land right after the `floodexposure-monitoring` chain writes the day's exposure at ~23:40–23:52 UTC, worst observed slip ~01:10) reads per-LGA FloodScan×WorldPop exposure from `app.floodscan_exposure` (prod DB), applies the strict 3-day rolling mean matching the threshold derivation, and evaluates the 4 retargeted LGAs against their framework thresholds. A freshness guard fails the run loudly (no email) if the latest `valid_date` is older than run-date−2. Three email states: trigger reached (→ `nga-flash:trigger` list 119), approaching threshold at ≥80% (→ `nga-flash:info` 118, any day), weekly Monday informational; `STAGE != prod` routes to `nga-flash:test` (120). The flash stream's audience is separate from the riverine lists. Operational runbook for both pipelines: [pipelines/nga-flooding-monitoring.md](../../pipelines/nga-flooding-monitoring.md).

## Key decisions & rationale

**Multi-gauge consensus over single-gauge OR logic.** The 2025 trigger's single Wuroboki station was a single point of failure. Requiring 6 of 10 gauges to agree simultaneously is a spatial quality filter that eliminates local noise and ensures the signal represents a basin-wide flood, at the cost of losing GloFAS as an independent model cross-check inside the action trigger — which is part of why GloFAS was redeployed as a separate readiness trigger instead.

**GloFAS readiness at 3,132 m³/s / LT≤13 d (option A), plus a reanalysis safety net.** The design note weighs option A against option B (3,250 m³/s, LT≤12 d): A has the shorter RP (3.0 vs 3.5 yr) and higher POD (0.75 vs 0.50) at a higher false-alarm rate (25% vs 19%), and is the only one that catches 2012 — though only by +2 days, effectively a nowcast. Neither option detects 2018 at any practical threshold within LT≤13 d, a GloFAS forecasting limitation rather than a threshold choice. Commit 5d44ff8 then added a reanalysis OR-branch against the same threshold "per the framework doc", so `evaluate_trigger` ORs a forecast-branch check with a near-real-time reanalysis check. (The prior sync framed this branch as something the repo analysis had evaluated and rejected; that framing could not be substantiated in the public notebooks — see `extra.readiness_trigger_design`.)

**Listmonk over raw SMTP.** Commit 2e5818f moved email dispatch to the team's shared Listmonk/ocha-relay convention (`infrastructure/comms-listmonk.md`) — tag-resolved lists instead of hardcoded ids, a shared branding/footer template, and a built-in test-mode banner, replacing bespoke SMTP + blob-CSV distribution-list plumbing.

**Adapting to the GloFAS v5 schema change without breaking calibration.** When CEMS restructured the `cems-glofas-historical` dataset in Aug 2026, the fetch code was updated to the new request schema but deliberately kept requesting `system_version: version_4_0` data — the 3,132 m³/s threshold's calibration is anchored to v4 reanalysis climatology, so silently picking up v5 data would have invalidated the threshold without a recalibration.

**Trying to ship ahead of the merge — but the shim didn't land.** Rather than wait for PR #37 to reach `main` (where GitHub Actions schedules must live), the team wrote a disposable shim workflow that checks out the feature branch directly, mirroring the pattern that already works for `deploy-app-cron.yml`. The shim was opened as its own PR (#38) and is still unmerged, so it inherits exactly the problem it was meant to solve: a `schedule:` on a non-default branch never fires. The 2026 design is therefore still untested in operation.

## Changes from previous version

The 2025-08-11 endorsed version uses a **single-station OR trigger** (GloFAS ≥3,132 m³/s OR Google GRRR ≥1,195 m³/s at Wuroboki) for the riverine action, plus a flash-flood observational trigger (FloodScan SFED per-LGA for 6 BAY LGAs). The 2026 version:

1. **Action trigger** — replaced single-station OR logic with a multi-gauge consensus trigger (≥6 of 10 GRRR gauges at 4-yr RP).
2. **Readiness trigger** — new GloFAS component: reforecast ensemble mean OR latest reanalysis, both vs 3,132 m³/s. Not present in the 2025 design.
3. **NHF flash flood** — retargeted from 6 LGAs to 4 (Mobbar, Maiduguri, Jere, Geidam; only Maiduguri carries over) with new framework-document exposure thresholds; monitoring live daily at 01:30 UTC with a new 80% advisory tier.
4. **Geographic scope** — Benue state, briefly scoped as a second target state in an earlier sync, was dropped; the 2026 riverine framework covers the 7 Adamawa LGAs only.
5. **Email dispatch** — moved from raw SMTP to Listmonk/ocha-relay (new since the prior sync of this page).
6. **GloFAS reanalysis OR-branch** — the prior sync's `[conflict]` (in the doc, absent from the code) is closed: commit 5d44ff8 implements it. The prior sync's added claim that the repo analysis had *rejected* it is unsourced and has been dropped.
7. **Monitoring season** — the 2026 document states 'June to end of December'; the 2026 calibration uses the Aug–Nov wet season (annual maxima).
8. **Funding** — envelope down from $7M (CERF $5M + NHF $2M) to **$6M** (CERF $5M + NHF $1M), plus $2M WFP co-financing; CERF recipients now WFP, IOM, UNICEF, WHO, FAO; NHF uses fewer partners (LESGO, NRCS, HARAF), with NRCS the single early-warning disseminator.
